About

What Is Bus Traffic Fever?

Bus Traffic Fever is a color-matching parking puzzle by GOODROID, Inc. You start with a board of colored buses and a queue of colored passenger groups. Your job is to dispatch each bus in the right order — send the bus whose color matches the group at the front of the queue into a parking slot, board the passengers, and clear every vehicle before the board locks up.

The hook is the chain reaction. Every dispatch changes which bus is exposed, so the "obvious" bus is rarely the right one to send first. Clear the wrong vehicle and you'll jam the slots; clear the right one and the whole board unwinds in a single satisfying sequence. That's why so many players stall on the same few levels and go hunting for a walkthrough.

How to Play

How to Play Bus Traffic Fever

Clear a board in five moves of thinking. Master the dispatch order and even the busiest queue becomes a straight line.

1

Read the front color

Look at the passenger group at the front of the queue first. That color tells you which bus to dispatch.

2

Dispatch the matching bus

Send the bus in that color to an open parking slot. It boards the group and frees the space.

3

Clear blocked buses in order

If the bus you need is parked behind another, clear the exposed one first so the path opens.

4

Re-read the queue

After every dispatch, check which color is now at the front. The next move always follows the queue.

5

Keep a slot open

Never fill the last parking slot with a color that is not up yet. If the board locks, restart and follow the queue.

Tips

Bus Traffic Fever Strategy Tips

The levels that trap most players aren't the biggest — they're the ones where the right first dispatch isn't obvious. These five habits unstick most of them.

1. Match the front color

The queue always tells you the next move. Dispatch the bus that matches the front group before anything else.

2. Think two dispatches ahead

A good dispatch exposes the next bus, not just the current one. If a clear only helps right now, look for a better first move.

3. Hold one slot open

Keeping a parking slot free gives you room to recover from a crowded board. Treat it as your escape route.

4. Clear repeats as they come

When a color returns for a second group, clear it in turn — do not batch the two together.

Hard levels

5. Use the guide as a dispatch check

On the known walls like 26, 28 and 40, pause after each dispatch and compare your board to the walkthrough before continuing.

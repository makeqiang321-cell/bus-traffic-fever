About

What Is Bus Traffic Fever?

Bus Traffic Fever is a mobile parking-jam puzzle by GOODROID, Inc. You start with a crowded lot where buses are jammed in every direction. Your job is to tap and slide them out in the right order — open the blocked lanes, collect the color-matched passengers waiting on the sidewalk, and clear every vehicle before the board locks up.

The hook is the chain reaction. Every move changes the traffic flow, so the "obvious" bus is rarely the right one to pull first. Free the wrong vehicle and you'll trap three more; free the right one and the whole jam unwinds in a single satisfying sequence. That's why so many players stall on the same few levels and go hunting for a walkthrough.

How to Play

How to Play Bus Traffic Fever

Clear a jam in five moves of thinking. Master the order and even the worst gridlock becomes a straight line.

1

Scan the whole board

Watch one full movement cycle before you tap. Find which bus can move without trapping another route.

2

Plan the first exit

Pick the exposed bus that frees the most space first — usually the one nearest an open lane.

3

Open lanes gradually

Clear the front spaces before you pull a deep bus from the back of the jam.

4

Collect by color

Steer each bus to its color-matched passengers to clear waiting spots and keep the flow going.

5

Avoid deadlocks

Never park a bus where it blocks two exits at once. If the board locks up, replay the sequence from step one.

Tips

Bus Traffic Fever Strategy Tips

The levels that trap most players aren't the biggest — they're the ones where the right first move isn't obvious. These five habits unstick most of them.

1. Start with exposed buses

Vehicles near an open lane almost always create the first safe route. Clear them before touching the packed middle.

2. Think two moves ahead

A good move opens the next bus, not just the current one. If a slide only helps you right now, look for a better first pull.

3. Protect the center lane

Keeping one lane open gives you room to recover from a crowded layout. Treat it as your escape route.

4. Match passengers early

Clearing a color group frees two things at once — the bus and the waiting spot. Don't leave riders standing.

Hard levels

5. Use the guide as a route check

On the known walls like 26, 28 and 180, pause after each major move and compare your board to the walkthrough before continuing.

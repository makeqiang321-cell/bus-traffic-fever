Bus Traffic Fever Level 11 Guide: The Bottleneck

Level 11 lives in the middle lane. Copy the central sequence from the video, ignore the tempting side lanes, and the rest of the board solves itself.

The Bottleneck in Level 11

A board where one central lane holds the whole level together. The side buses are easy, so the level is a test of not letting the easier-looking sides distract you from the middle.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Nail the middle-lane order and the sides fall into place. The central lane is the bottleneck, and the only hard part is the order you pull its buses in.

First Moves: Isolating the Middle Lane

  1. Focus only on the middle lane before anything else.
  2. Ignore the side buses even though they look easier.
  3. Copy the central bus order from the video.

Midgame: Finishing the Middle Lane

  1. With the middle half-cleared, keep its remaining order intact.
  2. Move the next middle bus only when the one before it is gone.
  3. Hold the middle's exit open until the last central bus leaves.

Finishing: Letting the Sides Follow

  1. Clear the middle lane completely first.
  2. Move the side buses once the bottleneck is open.
  3. Confirm the side lanes unwind without extra thought.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 11 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 11?+

Focus on the middle lane first and copy its bus order from the video. The side lanes fall into place once the middle is clear.

How do I know which lane is the middle in Bus Traffic Fever Level 11?+

It is the central lane that the side buses all connect to. The video's first few moves will make it obvious which one is the bottleneck.

Can I solve the sides before the middle in Bus Traffic Fever Level 11?+

No — the sides are easier but they depend on the middle opening first. Clearing the middle lane in order is what releases them.

Does the middle lane always matter most in Bus Traffic Fever Level 11?+

On this board, yes — it is the bottleneck. On other levels the key lane can be elsewhere, so identify it fresh each time.

Why does alternating sides and middle break the Bus Traffic Fever Level 11 sequence?+

It pulls the middle buses out of order, so the one correct sequence gets scrambled and the bottleneck never opens.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 43 Guide: The Eight-Vehicle Line

Level 43 is an eight-color line — pink, purple, green, red, brown, orange, yellow, blue — that runs through the VIP slot, five regular slots, and two plus slots. Do not rush the middle; each color has one correct slot and the level only clears if you keep the order.

The Eight-Vehicle Line in Level 43

A long eight-vehicle level with a pink lead and three slot types in play. It tests whether you can hold a color order across the longest queue you have seen.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Pink Into the VIP Slot

  1. Read the front color — it is pink, so the level opens on pink.
  2. Send the pink bus to the VIP slot. VIP is where the first group boards, and clearing it unlocks the regular slots.
  3. Confirm purple is next before you move it, so the long chain stays in order.

Midgame: Drain the Regular Slots

  1. Send purple, green, red, and brown into regular slots 1–4 in that order. Each one collects its group and frees the next slot.
  2. After brown boards, re-read the queue for orange — do not carry the order from memory.
  3. Line up yellow and blue for the plus slots, the last two colors of the run.

Finishing: The Plus Slots

  1. Send the orange bus to plus slot 1 to collect the orange group.
  2. Send the yellow bus to plus slot 2, then blue to plus slot 3.
  3. Confirm the level is clear once all eight groups have boarded.

Quick hint:

Hold the full order in your head — pink, purple, green, red, brown, orange, yellow, blue — and the eight vehicles clear themselves.

Common mistake:

Losing the order in the middle of the regular slots and firing red before green.

Pros and Cons

Pros. A satisfying long chain that proves you can hold a color sequence end to end.

Cons. Eight vehicles is a lot to track, and one skipped color breaks the whole line.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 43 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 43?+

Clear the eight vehicles in order: pink into the VIP slot, then purple, green, red, brown, orange, yellow, and blue through the regular and plus slots.

Which slot does the pink bus take in Bus Traffic Fever Level 43?+

The VIP slot. Pink is the front color and the VIP slot is the first free space.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 43 have so many vehicles?+

It is the first eight-vehicle level, built to test whether you can hold a full color order across three slot types.

What order do I clear the regular slots in Bus Traffic Fever Level 43?+

Purple, green, red, then brown. Orange, yellow, and blue follow into the plus slots.

Where does it go wrong in Bus Traffic Fever Level 43?+

In the middle of the regular slots, where players mix up green and red and break the sequence.

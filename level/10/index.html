⚡ Quick Answer

Clear the outer buses in queue order — blue, pink, then yellow — and the trapped inner buses open up. Bus layouts can shift between app updates, so treat the colors as the pattern, not a fixed script.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 10 Guide: The Corner Queue

Level 10 wraps the queue around the corner of the lot. Start with the outer blue bus, follow the queue through pink and yellow, and the inner buses come free as the perimeter empties.

The Corner Queue in Level 10

A level where the outer ring of buses boxes in the inner core. The queue runs blue, pink, then yellow, and you clear the outside first so the inside can reach the slots.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Clear the Outer Blue

  1. Blue leads — clear the blue group up front first.
  2. Pink is next in line, so take the pink group.
  3. Then yellow: let the yellow bus level its group.

Midgame: Open the Inner Ring

  1. The queue repeats yellow, so clear the second yellow group.
  2. Blue comes back around — free the remaining blue bus.
  3. As the outer buses leave, the center starts to open.

Finishing: Clear the Core

  1. Close the run with purple, then the last pink group.
  2. Make sure every group has boarded.
  3. Clear the level to finish.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 10 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 10?+

Clear the corner buses in queue order: blue, pink, yellow, yellow, blue, purple, then pink. Each dispatch opens the next corner.

Are the corners always the answer in Bus Traffic Fever Level 10?+

The queue points to the corners here. Most of the early buses are parked in the corners, so clearing them in order is the solve.

How many corners does Bus Traffic Fever Level 10 have?+

A handful of corner slots hold the first buses, and the center only frees up once those corners are cleared.

Why does the center in Bus Traffic Fever Level 10 only open after the corners?+

The center buses are parked behind the corner ones, so they stay blocked until the corners clear.

Why do the corners look out of the way in Bus Traffic Fever Level 10?+

They look parked off to the side, but they are first in the queue. Blue and pink open the corners, and the rest follows.

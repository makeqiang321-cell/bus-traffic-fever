Bus Traffic Fever Level 10 Guide: The Corner Holdout

Level 10 is won in the corners. Free the tucked-in edge buses first, let the middle open on its own, and do not touch the center until the corners are clear.

The Corner Holdout in Level 10

A board where the corners hold the most trapped vehicles. They look out of the way, but freeing them early is what unlocks the center — so the level is a test of edge-first discipline.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Work the corners before the center. The buses parked at the edges are the real blockers, and clearing them first gives the middle buses somewhere to go.

First Moves: Freeing the Corners

  1. Start at the corners and work inward.
  2. Clear each corner bus before touching the center.
  3. Let the edge lanes open fully.

Midgame: Spotting the Pivot

  1. Watch for the moment the last corner frees the center.
  2. Treat that moment as the cue to stop working the edges.
  3. Shift focus to the middle only after that cue fires.

Finishing: Letting the Middle Follow

  1. Once the corners are clear, the center buses free up.
  2. Move the middle buses into the lanes the corners opened.
  3. Confirm the edge-to-center order matches the video.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 10 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 10?+

Clear the corner buses first and work inward, leaving the middle until the edges are open. Match the video's edge-to-center order.

Are the corners always the answer in Bus Traffic Fever Level 10?+

On this board, yes — but it is not a universal rule. Watch whether the corners actually block the middle before applying it elsewhere.

How many corners does Bus Traffic Fever Level 10 have?+

Enough to trap the center. The key is that the corner buses block the middle, so clear them first regardless of the exact count.

Why does the center in Bus Traffic Fever Level 10 only open after the corners?+

Because the corner buses are what trap the middle vehicles. Until the edges are free, the center buses have no lane to move into, so the corners must go first.

Why do the corners look out of the way in Bus Traffic Fever Level 10?+

They sit at the edges, so they are easy to skip, but they hold the buses that trap the center. Clearing them early is what unlocks the middle.

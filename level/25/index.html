Bus Traffic Fever Level 25 Guide: The Slot Order

Level 25 clears in a clean six-color line: black, light blue, pink, brown, red, and purple. Each color takes the next slot in turn, so the order is the whole puzzle.

The Slot Order in Level 25

A board with six distinct colors queued in a straight sequence. The level is a pure ordering test — clear each color as it reaches the front and keep the slots matched.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Follow the order — black, light blue, pink, brown, red, purple. The straight sequence rewards simply clearing each color in turn.

First Moves: Clear Black and Light Blue

  1. Black is first — clear the black group.
  2. Light blue follows, so take it next.
  3. Pink comes up; board the pink group.

Midgame: Advance Brown and Red

  1. Brown is queued — clear it.
  2. Red follows, so take the red group.
  3. Re-read the front for the closing purple.

Finishing: Close With Purple

  1. Purple closes the run.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Quick hint:

Work the six colors straight through — black, light blue, pink, brown, red, purple.

Common mistake:

Pros and Cons

Pros. The straight order is one of the cleanest reads in the later game.

Cons. Its predictability makes rushing and skipping a slot more tempting.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 25 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 25?+

Hold the slot order: black, light blue, pink, brown, red, purple.

What is the opening order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 25?+

Black first, then light blue, pink, brown, red, purple. The opening tap is black.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 25 go wrong immediately?+

Because the wrong opening color jams the first slot. Start with black and the rest follows.

Do I tap the first bus fast in Bus Traffic Fever Level 25?+

Fast or slow, just make it black. The opening color is what matters, not the tap speed.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 25 all about the first move?+

Mostly the opening, black, light blue, pink, brown, red, purple, but every slot in the order counts.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 32 Guide: The Color Corridor

Level 32 runs a clean six-color corridor: pink, blue, yellow, orange, red, and green. Each clear opens the next bus, so the run is a straight front-to-back pass.

The Color Corridor in Level 32

A board where the buses line up in a corridor and clear in order. The queue runs pink, blue, yellow, orange, red, green, and the corridor empties one dispatch at a time.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Follow the order — pink, blue, yellow, orange, red, green. Clear the corridor front to back and the board drains cleanly.

First Moves: Clear Pink and Blue

  1. Dispatch the pink bus to clear the pink group.
  2. Dispatch the blue bus to board the blue group.
  3. Dispatch the yellow bus as yellow reaches the front.

Midgame: Work Orange and Red

  1. Dispatch the orange bus to board the orange group.
  2. Dispatch the red bus to clear the red group.
  3. Re-read the queue for the closing green.

Finishing: Close With Green

  1. Dispatch the green bus to board the green group.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 32 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 32?+

Walk the color corridor in order: pink, blue, yellow, orange, red, green.

What is the corridor order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 32?+

Pink, blue, yellow, orange, red, green. A straight line of six colors with no repeats.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 32 jam halfway?+

Because a color is grabbed out of order. The corridor is pink, blue, yellow, orange, red, green.

How do I keep the order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 32?+

Since there are no repeats, just move down the list: pink, blue, yellow, orange, red, green.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 32 harder than Level 26?+

Different. No repeated colors here, but the corridor is a full six deep, so it tests pure ordering.

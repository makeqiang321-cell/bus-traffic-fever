Bus Traffic Fever Level 32 Guide: The Spacing Corridor

Level 32 stretches the spacing lesson down a long corridor. Set one pace and carry it the whole length, one tap per bus, so the row clears evenly from front to back.

The Spacing Corridor in Level 32

A long row of buses that must be released in order. The corridor rewards a single steady tap pace, and the challenge is holding that spacing for every bus in the line.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

One steady pace across the whole corridor. Any rushed or double tap breaks the row and jams the lane.

First Moves: Setting the Corridor Pace

  1. Set one steady pace before the row starts.
  2. Give the front bus a single clean tap.
  3. Count the rhythm before the next bus.

Midgame: Spacing Down the Whole Row

  1. Space each bus one tap apart down the row.
  2. Do not speed up or double-tap mid-corridor.
  3. Keep the same pace from front to back.

Finishing: Clearing the Corridor's Tail

  1. Hold the pace through the last few buses.
  2. Tap each tail bus once, at its own pace.
  3. Finish once the whole corridor is clear.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 32 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 32?+

Hold one steady pace and tap each bus once, evenly spaced, down the whole corridor. Any rush or double-tap jams the row.

What is the corridor in Bus Traffic Fever Level 32?+

A long row of buses that must clear in order. The puzzle is holding an even tap spacing across the whole line.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 32 jam halfway?+

Because the tap pace sped up or a bus got double-tapped mid-row. Keep one steady rhythm from the front bus to the tail.

How do I keep the pace in Bus Traffic Fever Level 32?+

One tap per bus, one tap apart, from front to back. Match the video's steady spacing and do not rush the middle.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 32 harder than Bus Traffic Fever Level 26?+

Yes — same spacing discipline, but the longer corridor tests whether you can hold the pace to the very end.

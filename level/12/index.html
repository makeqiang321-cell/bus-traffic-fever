Bus Traffic Fever Level 12 Guide: The Big Clear

Level 12 clears in a tidy five-color order — blue, brown, pink, red, yellow. Start with the blue bus in the outer lane and each later color advances to the front in turn.

The Big Clear in Level 12

A board with five distinct colors queued in a clean sequence. The level is a straight ordering test: clear each color as it reaches the front and do not let a slot take the wrong one.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Work the queue in order — blue, brown, pink, red, yellow. Each clear pushes the next color forward, so dispatch front to front and the board empties itself.

First Moves: Clear the Outer Lane

  1. Blue is first — clear the leading blue group.
  2. Brown follows; take the brown group next.
  3. Pink comes up, so board the pink group.

Midgame: Advance Through Red

  1. Red is next in the queue — clear it.
  2. Leave a slot free for yellow.
  3. Re-read the front before the final move.

Finishing: Finish With Yellow

  1. Send yellow out last to take the yellow group.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Quick hint:

Clear in queue order — blue, brown, pink, red, yellow — and do not skip ahead.

Common mistake:

Skipping a color because a later bus is exposed, which parks the wrong bus in a slot and stalls the sequence.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The straight order makes it one of the easier mid-levels to read.

Cons. Its predictability tempts you to rush and dispatch out of order.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 12 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 12?+

Clear in this exact order: blue, brown, pink, red, yellow. The big clear only works when you dispatch them in sequence.

How do I count which bus frees more in Bus Traffic Fever Level 12?+

You don't have to count. The queue already orders it for you: blue first, then brown, pink, red, yellow.

What if two buses in Bus Traffic Fever Level 12 free the same number of spaces?+

Go by the queue, not by how many spaces they free. The front group's color is the tiebreaker every time.

Does the clear order really matter in Bus Traffic Fever Level 12?+

Yes. Blue, brown, pink, red, yellow is fixed; sending them out of order leaves the board half-cleared.

Does the Bus Traffic Fever Level 12 order change if I restart?+

No, the queue is the same each run, so blue, brown, pink, red, yellow holds every attempt.

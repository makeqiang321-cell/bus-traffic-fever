Bus Traffic Fever Level 49 Guide: The Double Orange

Level 49 is a hard seven-vehicle level with orange twice — once at the start, once in the middle. Run orange, brown, red, then the second orange, then yellow, blue, and green into the plus slot, and keep the two oranges from blurring together.

The Double Orange in Level 49

A hard seven-vehicle level where orange appears twice. The repeated color at the start and middle is the trap that separates the two halves.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Orange, Brown, Red

  1. Read the front color — it is orange, so the level opens on orange.
  2. Send the first orange bus to the VIP slot. VIP collects the first orange group and unlocks the regular slots.
  3. Send brown to regular slot 1 and red to regular slot 2. Each collects its group and frees the next slot.

Midgame: The Second Orange

  1. Re-read the queue — orange is back, and this second orange is its own passenger group.
  2. Send the second orange bus to regular slot 3. Do not skip it just because you already cleared an orange.
  3. Confirm yellow is next before moving, then line up blue behind it.

Finishing: Yellow, Blue, Green

  1. Send the yellow bus to regular slot 4 to collect the yellow group.
  2. Send the blue bus to regular slot 5 to collect the blue group.
  3. Send the green bus to plus slot 1 to collect the closing green group and clear the level.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 49 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 49?+

Clear orange, brown, red, then the second orange, then yellow and blue, and finish with green into the plus slot.

Why does orange appear twice in Bus Traffic Fever Level 49?+

Two orange passenger groups level at different times, so two orange buses clear — one at the start and one mid-queue.

Which slot does green take in Bus Traffic Fever Level 49?+

The plus slot, as the final dispatch after yellow and blue.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 49 harder than the boards before it?+

Yes. The repeated orange is a real trap, so read each wave carefully before you tap.

What is the common mistake in Bus Traffic Fever Level 49?+

Skipping the second orange after red, because it looks like the first orange you already cleared.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 8 Guide: The Blocked Duo

Level 8 sets up a potential deadlock: two buses boxed together. Dispatch the one whose color the queue wants, keep the other from filling the last slot, and the level stays live.

The Blocked Duo in Level 8

A level where two buses block each other and share a single escape slot. The level checks whether you can avoid the deadlock by dispatching in the order the queue demands.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Spot the Deadlock

  1. Find the two buses sharing one escape path.
  2. Read which of their colors is at the front of the queue.
  3. Dispatch that one first.

Midgame: Hold the Slot

  1. Keep the last shared slot open for the second bus.
  2. Do not park an unrelated color in it.
  3. Clear any bus that threatens to take that slot.

Finishing: Break the Duo

  1. Send the second bus once its color reaches the front.
  2. Confirm both buses have left the shared space.
  3. Finish before any new pair can form.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 8 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 8?+

Two buses block each other, so clear the one the queue calls for first. Dispatch its color and the pair unlocks.

Can I recover from a double-block in Bus Traffic Fever Level 8 without restarting?+

Only if a matching bus is still reachable. If you sent the wrong color first, restart, there is no undo.

How do I spot the blocked pair in Bus Traffic Fever Level 8?+

Find the two buses that cannot reach a slot because they are parked in front of each other, then serve whichever color the queue calls.

How do I undo a deadlock in Bus Traffic Fever Level 8?+

Serve the front group's color. That frees one of the blocked pair and the deadlock breaks.

Why did my Bus Traffic Fever Level 8 layout freeze even though I followed the video?+

A level can shift after an update, so re-read your on-screen queue. Your color order may differ from the recorded one.

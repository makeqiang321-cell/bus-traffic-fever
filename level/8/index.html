Bus Traffic Fever Level 8 Guide: The Double Block

Level 8 is about not making the deadlock move. Count how many exits each pull would close, skip anything that closes two, and the board stays solvable the whole way.

The Double Block in Level 8

A compact board with a couple of pinch points. If a bus settles into one of them, two exits close at the same time and the board locks — so the danger is a specific park, not a hard sequence.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Dodge the double-block. The level is forgiving until you park a bus where it blocks two exits at once, so avoiding that single mistake is the whole solve.

First Moves: Counting Exits Before You Park

  1. Before every move, count how many exits the bus would block.
  2. Skip any move that blocks two exits at once.
  3. Choose the pull that keeps one escape route open.

Midgame: Steering Clear of the Pinch

  1. Mid-solve, watch for the moment a pinch point starts to form.
  2. Route the next bus away from any spot that would block two exits.
  3. Keep one escape route open after every move.

Finishing: Keeping One Route Open

  1. After each move, confirm an escape route still exists.
  2. Avoid the pinch point even if it looks harmless.
  3. Finish once the last bus leaves without locking the board.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 8 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 8?+

Avoid parking any bus where it blocks two exits at once. Count the exits before each move and leave the double-block pulls alone.

Can I recover from a double-block in Bus Traffic Fever Level 8 without restarting?+

There is no undo, so the double-block is a restart. Keep one escape route open from the start and you will not hit it.

How do I spot the pinch point in Bus Traffic Fever Level 8?+

Look for the spot where one parked bus would close two exits at once. That is the pinch point, and the video's moves will show you which one to avoid.

How do I undo a deadlock in Bus Traffic Fever Level 8?+

There is no undo, so restart and keep one escape route open this time. The board is short enough that a restart is quick.

Why did my Bus Traffic Fever Level 8 board freeze even though I followed the video?+

A version difference likely changed one bus's position. Watch for the double-block moment and adjust rather than copying blindly.

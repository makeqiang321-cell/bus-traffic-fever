Bus Traffic Fever Level 31 Guide: The Opening Queue

Level 31 opens with green, runs purple and yellow, then returns to purple before closing on two greens. The first green starts the queue and the last two greens end it.

The Opening Queue in Level 31

A board where green appears three times across six groups. The queue runs green, purple, yellow, purple, green, green, and the doubled green at the end is the finisher.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Follow the order — green, purple, yellow, purple, green, green. Clear each group when it reaches the front, including the closing pair of greens.

First Moves: Open With Green

  1. Dispatch the green bus to board the first green group.
  2. Dispatch the purple bus to clear the purple group.
  3. Dispatch the yellow bus as yellow reaches the front.

Midgame: Return to Purple

  1. Dispatch the second purple bus to clear the next purple group.
  2. Dispatch the first closing green bus to board the green group.
  3. Re-read the queue for the last green.

Finishing: Close With Two Greens

  1. Dispatch the second closing green bus to board the green group.
  2. Dispatch the final green bus to clear the last green group.
  3. Confirm the board is empty to finish.

Quick hint:

Clear in order — green, purple, yellow, purple — then two greens to finish.

Common mistake:

Clearing the three greens together, since the middle purple and yellow are what keep the queue moving.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The closing double green gives a clear, strong finish.

Cons. Three greens scattered through the run are easy to miscount.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 31 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 31?+

Nail the opening queue: green, purple, yellow, purple, green, green.

What is the opening move in Bus Traffic Fever Level 31?+

Green. It leads the queue, so dispatch the green bus first.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 31 scramble at the start?+

Because green repeats three times and a wrong green goes in first. Start with green and hold the queue.

When do I dispatch the first bus in Bus Traffic Fever Level 31?+

As soon as you confirm it is green. The opening color is green, and the order is green, purple, yellow, purple, green, green.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 31 a harder opening board?+

Yes. Green repeating three times makes the opening harder to read than earlier levels.

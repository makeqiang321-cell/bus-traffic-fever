Bus Traffic Fever Level 31 Guide: The Opening Gate

Level 31 puts a gate on the opening move. Wait for the gate's short open window, release the first bus through it, and let that clean entry set the order for everything behind it.

The Opening Gate in Level 31

A board where the first bus must pass a gate that opens only briefly. The opening timing is tighter than before, and everything after it depends on hitting that window.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Hit the gate's open window with the first bus, and the board follows. Miss it, and the opening scramble costs the run.

First Moves: Watching the Gate's Window

  1. Watch the gate cycle before you move.
  2. Spot the short window when it is open.
  3. Release the first bus through that window.

Midgame: Letting the Clean Entry Set the Order

  1. Let the clean opening entry set the order.
  2. Do not scramble after a missed window.
  3. Keep the next buses in the opening's sequence.

Finishing: Closing on the Opening

  1. Follow the opening's order to the last buses.
  2. Keep the gate-timed order even to the finish.
  3. Finish once the gate-timed order clears.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 31 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 31?+

Watch the gate's cycle, then release the first bus through its open window. The clean entry sets the order for the whole board.

What is the gate in Bus Traffic Fever Level 31?+

A brief opening the first bus must pass through. The opening timing is about hitting the gate's open window, not just moving first.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 31 scramble at the start?+

Because the first bus hit a closed gate. Wait for the open window, then release once and let the order settle.

When do I tap the first bus in Bus Traffic Fever Level 31?+

At the gate's open window. Watch the cycle, match the video's release, and the opening will set the order cleanly.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 31 a harder opening-timing board?+

Yes — the gate makes the opening window tighter, so the first tap has to be more precise than earlier opening levels.

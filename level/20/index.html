Bus Traffic Fever Level 20 Guide: The Layered Queue

Level 20 layers purple and red through the queue: purple, red, red, then yellow, then purple twice to finish. Clear each layer as it reaches the front.

The Layered Queue in Level 20

A board where the queue stacks repeating colors in layers, so clearing by color alone leaves the wrong bus exposed. The level tests peeling each layer in the order the queue feeds it.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Follow the layered order — purple, red, red, yellow, purple, purple. The repeats are separate layers, so clear each group when it comes to the front.

First Moves: Peel the First Layers

  1. Dispatch the purple bus to board the first purple group.
  2. Dispatch the first red bus to clear the red group.
  3. Dispatch the second red bus as red returns.

Midgame: Clear the Yellow Layer

  1. Dispatch the yellow bus to board the yellow group.
  2. Re-read the queue for the closing purples.
  3. Keep a slot open for the remaining buses.

Finishing: Close With Two Purples

  1. Dispatch the first closing purple bus to clear the purple group.
  2. Dispatch the final purple bus to board the last purple group.
  3. Confirm the board is empty to finish.

Quick hint:

Peel the layers in order — purple, red, red, yellow, then two purples.

Common mistake:

Clearing both purples or both reds at once, which skips a layer and jams the middle.

Pros and Cons

Pros. A layered repeat that rewards peeling the queue one group at a time.

Cons. The stacked repeats make it easy to clear the wrong bus mid-board.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 20 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 20?+

Work the layered queue in order: purple, red, red, yellow, purple, purple.

How many layers should I split the Bus Traffic Fever Level 20 board into?+

None. Treat it as one queue of six: purple, red, red, yellow, purple, purple.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 20 a good stopping point?+

Sure. It closes out the earlier stretch with a repeated purple that rewards careful queue reading.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 20 the end of the easy section?+

Roughly. From here the queues get longer and colors repeat more, so lock in the habit now.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 20 feel overwhelming?+

Because purple repeats three times. It is not harder, just a longer queue: purple, red, red, yellow, purple, purple.

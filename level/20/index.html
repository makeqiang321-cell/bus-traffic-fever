Bus Traffic Fever Level 20 Guide: The Phased Grid

Level 20 closes the early game with a phased clear. Split the board into sections, finish each one fully before the next, and consolidate as you go so no half-cleared area blocks your next move.

The Phased Grid in Level 20

A dense end-of-phase board that breaks into smaller sections. It is large enough that a scattered approach turns it into a wall, but section by section it is just the habits from levels 1-19.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Clear in phases — finish one section before starting the next. Treating the big board as one mess is what makes it feel impossible; splitting it shrinks it to a normal solve.

First Moves: Dividing the Board

  1. Split the board into smaller sections mentally.
  2. Pick the section closest to an open exit.
  3. Finish that section fully before moving on.

Midgame: Consolidating Between Sections

  1. After a section clears, consolidate before starting the next.
  2. Sweep the cleared section for any bus left half-blocked.
  3. Pick the next section only after the last one is fully empty.

Finishing: Consolidating Section by Section

  1. Clear each section completely before the next.
  2. Consolidate as you go so nothing is left half-blocked.
  3. Confirm the phased order matches the video.

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 20?+

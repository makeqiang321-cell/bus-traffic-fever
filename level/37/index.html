Bus Traffic Fever Level 37 Guide: The Rolling Queue

Level 37 rolls green through the queue three times: green, purple, green, red, orange, and green to finish. Each green is a separate group, so clear them as they come.

The Rolling Queue in Level 37

A level where green appears three times across six groups. The queue runs green, purple, green, red, orange, green, and tracking the three greens is the whole puzzle.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Clear the First Green

  1. Green opens the run — clear it first.
  2. Purple is next, so take the purple group.
  3. Green returns; free the next green bus.

Midgame: Work Red and Orange

  1. Red comes up — clear it.
  2. Orange is queued, so board the orange group.
  3. Re-read the front for the closing green.

Finishing: Close With Green

  1. Green closes the run with the last green group.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the level is empty.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 37 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 37?+

Ride the rolling queue: green, purple, green, red, orange, green.

What makes the opening tricky in Bus Traffic Fever Level 37?+

Green appears three times, green, purple, green, red, orange, green, so the opening green is easy to mis-tap.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 37 stay stalled?+

A wrong green goes in. Serve green, purple, green, red, orange, green in order and it flows.

When do I release the first bus in Bus Traffic Fever Level 37?+

As soon as you confirm green. The first of three greens leads the queue.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 37 another opening level?+

Yes, and the triple green makes its opening the hardest part.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 41 Guide: The Purple Pair

Level 41 stretches the chain to five buses and hides one snag: two purple buses that must leave back to back. Fire black, blue, purple, purple, then green into the plus slot, and the level clears without a single stall.

The Purple Pair in Level 41

A five-vehicle level with a black bus, a blue bus, two purple buses, and a green bus. The passenger queue calls the same order, so it is a straight chain once you spot the paired purples.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Black, Then Blue

  1. Read the front passenger color — it is black.
  2. Send the black bus to the first open slot.
  3. Send the blue bus to the next slot while black boards.

Midgame: The Two Purples

  1. After blue clears, confirm the next two groups are both purple.
  2. Send the first purple bus to the third slot.
  3. Line up the second purple bus without breaking the queue.

Finishing: Green Into the Plus Slot

  1. Let the second purple bus finish boarding before you move.
  2. Send the green bus into the plus slot, not a regular one.
  3. Confirm all five passenger groups have boarded.

Quick hint:

The two purple buses have to leave back to back — do not slip another color between them.

Common mistake:

Firing the second purple bus out of order and jamming the queue. The pair must stay consecutive.

Pros and Cons

Pros. A clean five-step chain that drills the read-the-full-queue habit before the first tap.

Cons. The double purple looks like one repeated bus, so players lose track of which purple goes next.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 41 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 41?+

Dispatch the five buses in queue order — black, blue, purple, purple, then green into the plus slot. Keeping the two purples back to back is the whole level.

Why are there two purple buses in Bus Traffic Fever Level 41?+

Two passenger groups are purple, so two purple buses have to leave in a row. Sending any other color between them stalls the level.

Where does the green bus go in Bus Traffic Fever Level 41?+

Into the first plus slot. Keep the regular slots for the queue, then use the plus slot for the final green dispatch.

Does the black bus always leave first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 41?+

Yes. The front passenger group is black, so the black bus is the correct first dispatch. Blue follows, then the two purples.

What trips players up in Bus Traffic Fever Level 41?+

The paired purples. It is easy to fire one purple, then grab another color, and break the back-to-back order the queue asks for.

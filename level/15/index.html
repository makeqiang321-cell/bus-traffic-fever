Bus Traffic Fever Level 15 Guide: The Long Haul

Level 15 is a long, unforgiving route. Treat the video as a sequence to copy rather than a hint to skim, pause after every move, and never jump ahead to a bus the route has not reached yet.

The Long Haul in Level 15

A dense board where every bus depends on the last. One wrong entry collapses the whole route, which is why the level runs long and why it feels like the game's first genuine wall.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Follow the route order exactly, move by move. There is no shortcut — the route itself is the fastest path, and a single skipped move undoes the whole run.

First Moves: Committing to the Route

  1. Commit to the full route order before you start.
  2. Pause the video after each move and mirror it exactly.
  3. Never jump ahead to a bus the route has not reached.

Midgame: Managing the Long Middle

  1. In the long middle, protect your attention and pace yourself.
  2. Take the level in small chunks instead of one long run.
  3. If the route blurs, rewind the video rather than guessing a new order.

Finishing: Copying to the Last Move

  1. Keep mirroring the video one move at a time.
  2. Replay any move you are unsure about before continuing.
  3. Finish only after the final bus leaves in the recorded order.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 15 Walkthrough Solution

Green runs three times through a six-bus queue. Track the repeats — green, green, pink, green, light blue, dark blue — and clear each one in turn.

⚡ Quick Answer

Follow the order — green, green, pink, green, light blue, dark blue. The three greens are separate groups, so clear each one when it reaches the front.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 15 Guide: The Long Queue

Level 15 stretches the queue with green appearing three times. Dispatch the two opening greens, then pink, then the third green, and close with light blue and dark blue.

The Long Queue in Level 15

A level where the queue repeats green three times across six groups. The level tests whether you can track a color that keeps returning without clearing the wrong bus.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Clear Two Greens

  1. Green is up first — clear the opening green group.
  2. Green repeats right away, so take the next green group.
  3. Pink comes next; board the pink group.

Midgame: Clear the Third Green

  1. A third green returns later — hold for it.
  2. Leave light blue alone until it reaches the front.
  3. Re-read the queue for the closing colors.

Finishing: Light Blue, Dark Blue

  1. Clear light blue, then dark blue to finish.
  2. Confirm every group has boarded.
  3. Done — the long queue clears in order.

Quick hint:

Green repeats three times — clear each green group when it comes up, then finish with the two blues.

Common mistake:

Treating the three green groups as one, then clearing the wrong bus when green returns.

Pros and Cons

Cons. The repeated green makes the mid-game easy to lose track of.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 15 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 15?+

Work the long queue front to back: green, green, pink, green, light blue, dark blue.

How many moves does Bus Traffic Fever Level 15 take?+

Six dispatches, one per group in the queue: green, green, pink, green, light blue, dark blue.

What is the hardest single move in Bus Traffic Fever Level 15?+

The third green, because green shows up three times and it is easy to lose track of which green is next.

Is there a shortcut for Bus Traffic Fever Level 15?+

No. The long queue has no shortcut; you just serve it in order and the level clears.

Why is Bus Traffic Fever Level 15 so much harder than the rest?+

The queue is longer and green repeats three times, so tracking the order is the real test.

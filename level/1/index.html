Bus Traffic Fever Level 1 Guide: The First Match

The board is nearly empty — one or two buses and a single colored passenger group. Read which color is waiting first, send the matching bus to a slot, and the level clears itself.

The First Match in Level 1

A near-empty lot with one or two buses and a single passenger group. There is nothing to untangle — it just checks that you can read the front passenger's color and send the right bus to the slot.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Match the bus to the front passenger color and stop. If your first dispatch matches, the level is already clear.

First Moves: Read the Queue

  1. Watch the first passenger group and note its color.
  2. Find the bus in that same color.
  3. Dispatch it to the open parking slot.

Midgame: Confirm the Match

  1. Check that the bus color matches the group at the front.
  2. Let the passengers board and the slot empty.
  3. If a second bus is waiting, note its color before touching it.

Finishing: Clear the Board

  1. Send any remaining matching bus to the next open slot.
  2. Confirm every passenger group has boarded.
  3. Run the opening once more so the read-then-dispatch habit sticks.

Quick hint:

Read the front passenger's color before you touch any bus.

Common mistake:

Dispatching a bus whose color is not the one at the front. The slot takes the wrong passengers and the board stalls.

Pros and Cons

Pros. Fast to clear and easy to replay — a low-stakes way to lock in the color-matching habit.

Cons. So simple that players skip it, then misread the queue on later levels.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 1 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 1?+

Match the bus to the passenger color at the front of the queue, then dispatch it to its slot. Level 1 is the first match, so one correct bus clears the board.

Do I need to watch the whole Bus Traffic Fever Level 1 video?+

No, the first few seconds show the only move. Level 1 is a single match, so the opening is the whole lesson.

Why is Bus Traffic Fever Level 1 important if it is so easy?+

It teaches the one rule every later level uses: the bus color has to match the passenger group waiting at the front. Get that and the rest is just longer queues.

Do I need to unlock anything before Bus Traffic Fever Level 1?+

No, Level 1 is open from the start. If it is not showing, your app may be a limited demo, so check the store listing.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 1 just a tutorial?+

Pretty much. It introduces the color-matching loop with almost no traffic on the board, a warm-up rather than a puzzle.

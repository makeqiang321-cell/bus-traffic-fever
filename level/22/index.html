Bus Traffic Fever Level 22 Guide: The Queue Opener

Level 22 opens with red and brings it back halfway: red, blue, green, red, then yellow and purple to finish. The first red frees the first slot and the second red reopens the queue.

The Queue Opener in Level 22

A level where the opening color returns mid-sequence. The queue runs red, blue, green, red, yellow, and purple, and the two reds bookend the middle.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Clear the First Red

  1. Red opens the run — clear it first.
  2. Blue follows, so take the blue group.
  3. Green comes next; board the green group.

Midgame: Return to Red

  1. Red returns to the front — free the next red bus.
  2. Yellow is queued, so clear it.
  3. Keep a slot open for the closing purple.

Finishing: Close With Purple

  1. Purple closes the level — take it last.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the level is empty.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 22 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 22?+

Open with the queue: red, blue, green, red, yellow, purple. The opening move is red.

What does the opening queue look like in Bus Traffic Fever Level 22?+

Red leads, then blue, green, red, yellow, purple. Read it straight down and the level clears.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 22 keep ending on its own?+

A wrong dispatch blocks every bus behind it, so the level locks. Serving the queue order prevents it.

Which bus do I clear first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 22?+

The red bus. It matches the front group and opens the whole sequence.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 22 the first opening-queue level?+

It is one of the first to make you read a six-deep queue right from the opening tap.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 2 Guide: The Twin Exit Trap

Level 2 is your first real decision: which bus leaves first. Choose the exit that leaves the second lane free, ignore the closer bus, and the board clears itself from there.

The Twin Exit Trap in Level 2

A two-lane board where two buses both look ready to move. Only one opens the passenger route without trapping the second vehicle — the closer bus is usually the wrong one.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Pick the bus that does not block the other lane. The correct first move is the one that keeps a second exit open, not the one that looks closest.

First Moves: Choosing the Right Exit

  1. Compare the two exposed buses side by side.
  2. Pick the one whose exit leaves the second lane free, not the closest.
  3. Pull it and watch the first lane open cleanly.

Midgame: Facing the Second Lane

  1. After the first exit clears, ignore it and face the second lane.
  2. Confirm the second bus is no longer boxed in by the first.
  3. Line the second bus up while the first lane is still empty.

Finishing: Following the Second Bus

  1. Let the first bus clear fully before moving again.
  2. Send the second bus out the lane you kept open.
  3. Confirm both exits have emptied before you call it done.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 2 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 2?+

Choose the bus that leaves a second lane free rather than the one that looks closest, then follow the video's first-selection order.

In Bus Traffic Fever Level 2, what if both buses look identical?+

Look at which exit stays open after each possible move. The correct bus is the one whose exit does not seal the other lane.

Does the wrong first pick in Bus Traffic Fever Level 2 force a restart?+

Usually yes — it blocks the second lane and there is no undo, so restart and pick the non-blocking exit the second time.

Does the Bus Traffic Fever Level 2 two-lane trick work on later levels too?+

The habit — read before you pull — carries over, but later boards add more buses and colors, so the exact two-lane setup rarely repeats.

Why do I keep grabbing the wrong bus first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 2?+

Because the closer bus is the natural reflex. Slow down, compare both exits, and let the video's first selection break the habit.

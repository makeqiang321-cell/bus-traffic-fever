Bus Traffic Fever Level 13 Guide: The Color Maze

Level 13 makes the color groups the whole puzzle. Read every group first, pick the collection order that keeps the most lanes open, then execute it without improvising.

The Color Maze in Level 13

A denser board where color groups overlap across lanes. You need a collection plan rather than a first move, because collecting in the order you see them is exactly what fails.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Plan the full color order before your first move. The wrong first collection blocks a lane you still need, so the level is won or lost in the planning.

First Moves: Reading the Whole Board

  1. Read every color group before you move a single bus.
  2. Pick the collection order that keeps the most lanes open.
  3. Commit to that order before your first pull.

Midgame: Holding the Plan Under Pressure

  1. When a lane tightens mid-plan, do not improvise.
  2. Stick to the collection order you committed to.
  3. Pause and re-read rather than grabbing a closer bus.

Finishing: Executing Without Improvising

  1. Execute the planned order exactly — no improvising.
  2. Watch the video's first two collections if you get stuck.
  3. Finish once the last group clears without a blocked lane.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 13 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 13?+

Read every color group before moving and pick a collection order that keeps the most lanes open, then execute it without improvising.

How many color groups are on the Bus Traffic Fever Level 13 board?+

Several, and they overlap across lanes — which is why you need a full plan instead of a single first move. The video shows the exact order.

Is there a wrong way to plan the color order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 13?+

Yes — any order that blocks a lane you still need. The right order is the one that keeps the most lanes open at every step.

Why is Bus Traffic Fever Level 13 harder than the levels before it?+

The color groups overlap across lanes, so a wrong first collection order blocks a lane you still need. It is the first level that demands a full plan.

How do I choose the right color order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 13?+

Prefer the order that keeps the most lanes open at every step. Watch the video's first two collections to see which group unlocks the board.

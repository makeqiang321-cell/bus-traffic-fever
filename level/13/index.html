Bus Traffic Fever Level 13 Guide: The Color Maze

Level 13 repeats blue across the sequence, which is what makes it a maze. The queue runs blue, yellow, green, then blue again, then purple, then blue to finish.

The Color Maze in Level 13

A board where blue appears three separate times in the queue, so you cannot clear by color once and move on. The level forces you to re-read which blue bus is exposed each round.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Open With Blue

  1. Blue is up first — clear the blue group.
  2. Yellow follows, so take the yellow group.
  3. Green comes next; board the green group.

Midgame: Find the Second Blue

  1. Blue returns to the front — free the next blue bus.
  2. Leave purple alone until it is actually up.
  3. Re-check the queue after each clear.

Finishing: Purple, Then Blue

  1. Clear purple, then the third and last blue group.
  2. Confirm the board is empty to finish.
  3. Done — the repeated blue was the whole trick.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 13 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 13?+

Follow the color maze in order: blue, yellow, green, blue, purple, blue. Blue appears three times, so read carefully.

How many color groups are on the Bus Traffic Fever Level 13 board?+

Six groups, with blue repeating three times. That repetition is what makes the maze tricky.

Is there a wrong way to plan the color order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 13?+

Yes. Grabbing any blue bus early breaks it, because blue shows up three separate times in the sequence.

Why is Bus Traffic Fever Level 13 harder than the levels before it?+

A repeated color means you have to track the queue, not just hunt for a matching bus.

How do I choose the right color order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 13?+

Stick to blue, yellow, green, blue, purple, blue. Each blue is a different bus in a different spot.

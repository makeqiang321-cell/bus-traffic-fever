Bus Traffic Fever Level 9 Guide: The Cross Pickup

Level 9 crosses the level: the passenger group you need routes to a bus on the opposite side. Read the color, find its bus wherever it sits, and dispatch it.

The Cross Pickup in Level 9

A level where the queue's colors jump around rather than lining up in order. The test is tracing each front color to its matching bus, even when it is on the far side.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Trace the First Color

  1. Read the front color and trace it to its bus.
  2. Do not assume it is the nearest vehicle.
  3. Dispatch the matching bus wherever it sits.

Midgame: Work the Cross

  1. After each clear, re-trace the next front color.
  2. Dispatch the matching bus even if it is across the level.
  3. Keep slots open so the far-side buses can reach them.

Finishing: Clear the Far Side

  1. Clear the remaining buses by color, ignoring their position.
  2. Confirm each group has boarded in queue order.
  3. Finish once the level is empty.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 9 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 9?+

Buses cross the level to reach their slots, so dispatch the front group's color and let it cross cleanly. One wrong cross blocks the rest.

Do the passengers in Bus Traffic Fever Level 9 have to match the bus color exactly?+

Yes, a bus only picks up the passenger group of its own color, so exact matching is the whole puzzle.

What if two groups are the same color in Bus Traffic Fever Level 9?+

Serve them in queue order, front group first. Both need the same bus color, but the order is fixed.

Why do my pickups keep crossing in Bus Traffic Fever Level 9?+

Because a bus is heading to the wrong slot. Match the front group and each bus crosses to its own slot without colliding.

Does Bus Traffic Fever Level 9 need more speed or more planning?+

More reading. The queue already plans the route for you; speed is not what unlocks a cross pickup.

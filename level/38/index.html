Bus Traffic Fever Level 38 Guide: The Gridlock Spacer

Level 38 turns a gridlock into a spacing puzzle. Set one steady pace and release each bus a tap apart, letting the even taps unwind the jam instead of spamming it.

The Gridlock Spacer in Level 38

A gridlocked board where the only way out is disciplined input. Each bus needs one tap spaced a tap apart, and spam or double-taps only tighten the jam.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Break the gridlock with even tap spacing. One tap per bus, one tap apart, and the jam unwinds in order.

First Moves: Setting the Unwind Pace

  1. Set one steady pace before touching the jam.
  2. Release the first bus with a single tap.
  3. Count the rhythm before the next release.

Midgame: Unwinding the Gridlock

  1. Release each bus one tap apart.
  2. Do not spam or double-tap mid-jam.
  3. Let the even spacing unwind the gridlock.

Finishing: Clearing the Unwound Jam

  1. Hold the spacing through the last buses.
  2. Tap each final bus once, on its rhythm.
  3. Finish once the gridlock fully clears.

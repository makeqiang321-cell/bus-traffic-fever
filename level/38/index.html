Bus Traffic Fever Level 38 Guide: The Gridlock Clear

Level 38 opens with purple, runs orange, then purple again before finishing on brown, red, and blue. Catching the second purple is the key to clearing the gridlock.

The Gridlock Clear in Level 38

A level where purple appears twice with orange between them. The queue runs purple, orange, purple, brown, red, blue, and the second purple clears the middle.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Clear the First Purple

  1. Purple is up first — clear it.
  2. Orange follows, so take the orange group.
  3. Purple returns; free the purple bus again.

Midgame: Work Brown and Red

  1. Brown comes up — clear it.
  2. Red is queued, so take the red group.
  3. Re-read the front for the closing blue.

Finishing: Close With Blue

  1. Blue closes the level.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the level is empty.

Quick hint:

Catch the second purple after orange — the order is purple, orange, purple, brown, red, blue.

Common mistake:

Clearing both purples together, which skips orange and jams the middle of the gridlock.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The returning purple is a clear signal for the halfway point.

Cons. Players who batch the two purples stall the middle of the run.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 38 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 38?+

Break the gridlock in order: purple, orange, purple, brown, red, blue.

What is the gridlock in Bus Traffic Fever Level 38?+

Buses parked in front of each other, and only the queue order, purple, orange, purple, brown, red, blue, unlocks them.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 38 get worse when I tap fast?+

Because speed grabs the wrong color and deepens the gridlock. Read the queue instead of rushing.

How do I break the jam in Bus Traffic Fever Level 38?+

Serve the queue front to back, purple, orange, purple, brown, red, blue, and each clear opens the next bus.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 38 the hardest clear level?+

One of them. The repeated purple plus a locked grid makes it a real test of order discipline.

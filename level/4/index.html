Bus Traffic Fever Level 4 Guide: The Lifeline Lane

Level 4 teaches you to keep one lane open at all times. Identify the critical lane before you move, and never park a bus across it, no matter how safe the move looks.

The Lifeline Lane in Level 4

A board with one critical lane that feeds most of the exits. If that lane closes, the remaining buses have nowhere to go — so the level is really a test of protection, not speed.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Protect the one lane that matters. Once you never let a bus settle across it, every other move becomes easy to plan and the board never freezes.

First Moves: Finding the Lane That Matters

  1. Look for the lane that touches the most buses and exits at once.
  2. Treat that lane as protected before you make a single move.
  3. Plan your first pull around keeping it clear.

Midgame: Checking the Lifeline

  1. Mid-solve, stop and look only at the critical lane.
  2. Confirm no moved bus has drifted across it.
  3. Clear the one bus threatening it before making any other pull.

Finishing: Defending the Lane to the End

  1. After every move, check that the protected lane is still open.
  2. Refuse any pull that would park a bus across it.
  3. Finish only when the last bus leaves without closing the lane.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 4 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 4?+

Identify the single lane that feeds most exits and never park a bus across it. Plan every pull around keeping that route free.

How many lanes does Bus Traffic Fever Level 4 have?+

Several, but only one of them feeds most of the exits. The level is about spotting that key lane, not managing all of them equally.

What is the biggest time-waster in Bus Traffic Fever Level 4?+

Blocking the key lane and having to restart. A single wrong park undoes the run, so protecting the lane is faster than playing quickly.

What happens if I block the key lane in Bus Traffic Fever Level 4?+

The board freezes because the remaining buses lose their only exit route. You will have to restart and keep that lane clear the second time.

How do I spot the key lane quickly in Bus Traffic Fever Level 4?+

Look for the lane that touches the most buses and exits at once. That is the one to protect, and the video's early moves will confirm it.

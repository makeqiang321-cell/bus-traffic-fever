Bus Traffic Fever Level 40 Guide: The Last Queue

Level 40 runs the final queue in a clean six-color line: green, orange, purple, blue, red, and yellow. Clear each color in order and the level empties with no bus left behind.

The Last Queue in Level 40

A level where the final six colors clear in sequence. The queue runs green, orange, purple, blue, red, yellow, and the level is a straight ordering test to close the game.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Open With Green

  1. Green opens the final run — clear it first.
  2. Orange is next, so take the orange group.
  3. Purple follows; board the purple group.

Midgame: Clear Blue and Red

  1. Blue is queued — clear it.
  2. Red follows, so take the red group.
  3. Re-read the front for the closing yellow.

Finishing: Close With Yellow

  1. Yellow closes the game.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the level is empty.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 40 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 40?+

Finish the last queue: green, orange, purple, blue, red, yellow.

What is the final queue in Bus Traffic Fever Level 40?+

Green, orange, purple, blue, red, yellow. Six colors, no repeats, the clean finish to the game.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 40 feel like a wall?+

Because it is the last level and players rush it. It is just one clean queue: green, orange, purple, blue, red, yellow.

Which bus do I clear first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 40?+

Green, then orange, purple, blue, red, yellow.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 40 a boss level?+

Bus Traffic Fever Level 60 Guide: The Seven-Plus Finish

Level 60 leans almost entirely on the plus slots. Yellow opens into the VIP slot, then green, red, purple, blue, orange, brown, and pink fill the seven plus slots in order. Hold the long plus-slot run and the level clears cleanly.

The Seven-Plus Finish in Level 60

An eight-bus level with seven plus slots in a row after the yellow VIP lead. It is a long ordering test built almost entirely on one slot type.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Yellow Into the VIP Slot

  1. Read the front color — it is yellow, so the level opens on yellow.
  2. Send the yellow bus into the VIP slot. VIP collects the yellow group first.
  3. Confirm green is next before moving — seven plus slots run after this.

Midgame: The Plus-Slot Run

  1. Send green, red, purple, and blue into plus slots 1–4 in order. Each plus slot collects its group and keeps the next color from blocking.
  2. Re-read the queue after blue boards — the run is long, so do not lose count.
  3. Line up orange for plus slot 5.

Finishing: Orange, Brown, Pink

  1. Send orange, brown, and pink into plus slots 5–7 in order.
  2. Confirm each color boards before the next dispatch, so the long run stays straight.
  3. Check that all eight groups have cleared and the level is empty.

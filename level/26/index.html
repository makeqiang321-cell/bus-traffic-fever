Bus Traffic Fever Level 26 Guide: The Color Sequence

Level 26 runs blue at both ends of the sequence: blue, red, yellow, orange, purple, and blue to finish. The first blue frees the first slot and the last blue clears the board.

The Color Sequence in Level 26

A board where blue bookends the queue. The sequence is blue, red, yellow, orange, purple, blue, and the level tests clearing the same color early and late.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Follow the order — blue, red, yellow, orange, purple, blue. The two blues are separate groups, so clear each when it reaches the front.

First Moves: Clear the First Blue

  1. Dispatch the first blue bus to clear space and board the blue group.
  2. Dispatch the red bus to clear the red group.
  3. Dispatch the yellow bus to board the yellow group.

Midgame: Work Orange and Purple

  1. Dispatch the orange bus to clear the orange group.
  2. Dispatch the purple bus to board the purple group.
  3. Hold a slot for the closing blue.

Finishing: Close With Blue

  1. Dispatch the final blue bus to board the blue group.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Quick hint:

Clear in order — blue, red, yellow, orange, purple — then the final blue.

Common mistake:

Clearing both blues at the start and finding the final group has no bus left to board it.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The bookended blue makes the run easy to visualize end to end.

Cons. Easy to over-clear blue early and strand the closing group.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 26 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 26?+

Follow the color sequence: blue, red, yellow, orange, purple, blue.

What is the color sequence in Bus Traffic Fever Level 26?+

Blue, red, yellow, orange, purple, then blue again. Blue repeats, so track the queue.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 26 keep locking up?+

The repeated blue. Grabbing either blue out of order jams the board. Serve blue, red, yellow, orange, purple, blue.

How fast should I go in Bus Traffic Fever Level 26?+

Speed is not the point. Read the sequence blue, red, yellow, orange, purple, blue and go at that pace.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 26 harder than the earlier boards?+

A touch, because blue repeats and the queue is six deep, but it is the same read-the-queue rule.

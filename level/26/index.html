Bus Traffic Fever Level 26 Guide: The Tap Spacer

Level 26 is about the gap between taps. Set a steady pace, give every bus one tap exactly one tap apart, and never double-tap a bus while the one before it is still moving.

The Tap Spacer in Level 26

A board that punishes rushed input. Each bus needs its own rhythm, and the puzzle is holding that spacing steady instead of tapping as fast as you can.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

One tap per bus, evenly spaced. The level fails the moment you spam or double-tap and the queue falls out of step.

First Moves: Setting a Steady Tap Pace

  1. Set one steady pace before you tap anything.
  2. Give the first bus a single clean tap.
  3. Count the rhythm before the second tap.

Midgame: Spacing Each Bus Apart

  1. Space every bus exactly one tap apart.
  2. Do not double-tap while the previous bus moves.
  3. Keep the same pace through the middle.

Finishing: Holding the Spacing to the End

  1. Hold the steady pace for the final buses.
  2. Tap each last bus once, at its own pace.
  3. Finish without a single rushed double-tap.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 26 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 26?+

Hold a steady pace and give each bus one tap, evenly spaced. Do not double-tap or spam, and the queue will clear in order.

What is 'controlled tap spacing' in Bus Traffic Fever Level 26?+

Tapping each bus one tap apart instead of spamming. The spacing between taps is what keeps the queue in step.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 26 keep locking up?+

Because a double-tap or a rush of taps threw the queue out of step. Slow to one steady rhythm and space every tap.

How fast should I tap in Bus Traffic Fever Level 26?+

Steady, not fast. One tap per bus, evenly spaced — match the video's pace rather than tapping on instinct.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 26 harder than the earlier boards?+

Yes — it moves into the Medium range and tests input discipline, not just which bus to move.

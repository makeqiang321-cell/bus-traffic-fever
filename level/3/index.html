Bus Traffic Fever Level 3 Guide: The Front Line

Level 3 separates the front row from the back row for the first time. Empty the front in order, leave the buried buses alone until their path is free, and the stage unwinds on its own.

The Front Line in Level 3

A shallow board with a clear front row and a couple of buses tucked behind it. It is the first level where the order you clear that front row actually decides the result.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

The safe path runs through the front of the lot. Pull the buses nearest the open edge first, and the deeper vehicles come loose once the row in front of them is gone.

First Moves: Clearing the Front Row

  1. Work left to right (or right to left) along the front row.
  2. Pull only the buses nearest the open edge.
  3. Stop and check before you reach for anything deeper.

Midgame: Reading What the Front Revealed

  1. Once the front row is gone, pause and read the buses behind it.
  2. Identify which back bus now has a straight line to an exit.
  3. Set up the newly exposed bus before touching any remaining front vehicle.

Finishing: Freeing the Back Buses

  1. Once the front bus is gone, the back bus behind it opens up.
  2. Move the newly exposed back bus into the lane you just cleared.
  3. Confirm the full front-to-back order matches the video.

Quick hint:

Common mistake:

Bus Traffic Fever Level 3 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 3?+

Clear the front row first and only touch a back bus after the one in front of it has moved. Follow the video's front-to-back order.

In Bus Traffic Fever Level 3, how do I know which row is the front?+

The front row is the set of buses closest to the open edge or exit. The back row is the one tucked behind them.

Can I clear the back row first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 3?+

No — the back buses are physically blocked by the front row, so they cannot move until the bus in front of them has left.

Why do the back buses not move at first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 3?+

They are blocked by the front row. Until the bus in front of them moves, the rear vehicles have no lane to exit through.

Does front-row order really matter on Bus Traffic Fever Level 3's small board?+

Yes — this is the level that introduces the rule, so clearing the row out of order will stall the board even though it looks tiny.

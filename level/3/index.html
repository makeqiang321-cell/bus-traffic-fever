Bus Traffic Fever Level 3 Guide: The Slot Opener

Level 3 separates the exposed bus from the blocked bus for the first time. Clear the one that matches the front passenger color, leave the buried bus alone until its path is free, and the stage unwinds on its own.

The Slot Opener in Level 3

A shallow board with a clear front bus and a couple of vehicles tucked behind it. It is the first level where clearing the exposed matching bus is what uncovers the rest.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

The safe path runs through the exposed bus. Dispatch the one that matches the front color first, and the deeper buses come loose once the space in front of them is gone.

First Moves: Clear the Exposed Bus

  1. Read the front passenger color.
  2. Find the exposed bus in that color.
  3. Dispatch it and free the slot it was blocking.

Midgame: Read What Opened Up

  1. Once the first bus is gone, pause and read the buses behind it.
  2. Identify which newly exposed bus now has a clear path to a slot.
  3. Check its color against the queue before you send it.

Finishing: Free the Blocked Buses

  1. Move the newly exposed bus into the slot you cleared.
  2. Keep matching each bus to the color at the front of the queue.
  3. Confirm the full board order matches the video.

Quick hint:

Clear the exposed matching bus before you chase one parked behind it.

Common mistake:

Sending a blocked bus early because it looks tempting — it cannot reach a slot until the bus in front of it has moved.

Pros and Cons

Pros. Fast and linear once you spot the exposed bus — a confidence builder.

Cons. Looks so open that players reach into the back early and jam it up.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 3 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 3?+

Dispatch the matching-color bus to its numbered slot, which opens the slot behind it. Clear the buses in the order the queue calls for.

In Bus Traffic Fever Level 3, how do I know which bus to send first?+

Read the front passenger group. Its color points to the exact bus to dispatch, and the slot opens once that bus leaves.

Can I clear the back buses first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 3?+

No, a back bus stays blocked until the bus in front of it has been dispatched to its slot.

Why do the back buses not move at first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 3?+

They are parked behind another bus, so they have no slot to reach until the bus ahead of them clears.

Does the dispatch order really matter on Bus Traffic Fever Level 3's small board?+

Yes, this is the level that introduces slot opening, so sending buses out of queue order stalls the board even though it looks tiny.

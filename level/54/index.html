Bus Traffic Fever Level 54 Guide: The Hard Seven

Level 54 is a hard seven-car level with a long regular-slot run. Brown opens into the VIP slot, then orange, red, green, and pink fill regular slots 1–4, and yellow and blue finish through plus slots 1–2. Hold the order and do not let the length rush you.

The Hard Seven in Level 54

A hard seven-car level that runs a long regular-slot sequence before the plus slots. It is a straight order, but the length makes skipping a color easy.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Brown Into the VIP Slot

  1. Read the front color — it is brown, so the level opens on brown.
  2. Send the brown bus to the VIP slot. VIP collects the brown group and unlocks the regular slots.
  3. Confirm orange is next before moving, so the long run stays in order.

Midgame: Orange, Red, Green, Pink

  1. Send orange, red, green, and pink into regular slots 1–4 in order. Each collects its group and frees the next slot.
  2. Re-read the queue after pink boards — do not carry the order from memory.
  3. Hold plus slots 1–2 for yellow and blue, the last two colors.

Finishing: Yellow and Blue

  1. Send the yellow bus to plus slot 1 to collect the yellow group.
  2. Send the blue bus to plus slot 2 to collect the closing blue group.
  3. Confirm all seven groups have boarded and the level is clear.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 54 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 54?+

Clear brown into the VIP slot, then orange, red, green, and pink into regular slots 1–4, and finish with yellow and blue in plus slots 1–2.

Which car opens Bus Traffic Fever Level 54?+

Brown, into the VIP slot. Orange, red, green, and pink follow through the regular slots.

Where do yellow and blue go in Bus Traffic Fever Level 54?+

Into the two plus slots, in that order, to finish the level.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 54 hard?+

Yes. The four-slot regular run is long enough that a single skipped color stalls the whole level.

What is the mistake to avoid in Bus Traffic Fever Level 54?+

Skipping a color in the orange-red-green-pink run because you are moving too fast.

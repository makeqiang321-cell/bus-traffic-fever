Bus Traffic Fever Level 42 Guide: The Slot Chain

Level 42 is a straight five-color chain — brown, yellow, blue, pink, red — where every vehicle after the first lands in a plus slot. Read the order once and fire each bus as its color reaches the front, and the level clears without a single rethink.

The Slot Chain in Level 42

A five-vehicle level where the brown bus leads into the VIP slot and the rest feed the plus slots. It is a pure order test with no blocked vehicles to work around.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Brown Into the VIP Slot

  1. Read the front passenger color — it is brown, so brown leads the whole line.
  2. Send the brown bus to the VIP slot. This is the only slot that collects the brown group, so it clears the front of the queue.
  3. Watch the slot empty before the next dispatch — that empty slot is what lets yellow level next.

Midgame: Feed the Plus Slots

  1. After brown boards, confirm yellow is next. Matching the front color is what pushes the queue forward.
  2. Send the yellow bus to plus slot 1. The extra plus slot keeps yellow from blocking blue behind it.
  3. Line up blue for plus slot 2, and keep pink and red ready behind it.

Finishing: Pink and Red

  1. Send the pink bus to plus slot 3 to collect the pink group.
  2. Send the red bus to plus slot 4 to collect the final red group.
  3. Confirm all five groups have boarded and the level is clear.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 42 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 42?+

Dispatch the five buses in order — brown into the VIP slot, then yellow, blue, pink, and red into the next four plus slots.

Which slot does the brown bus take in Bus Traffic Fever Level 42?+

The VIP slot. It is the first free slot, and brown is the color waiting at the front of the queue.

Do all the other buses go to plus slots in Bus Traffic Fever Level 42?+

Yes. Yellow, blue, pink, and red each take the next open plus slot in that order.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 42 feel harder than it is?+

Because four plus slots in a row look cluttered. It is still one color order — brown, yellow, blue, pink, red.

Can I change the order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 42?+

No. Each bus has to match the passenger group at the front, so the order stays brown, yellow, blue, pink, red.

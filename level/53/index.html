Bus Traffic Fever Level 53 Guide: The Cyan Split

Level 53 spreads six buses across regular, VIP, and plus slots. Run yellow and red into regular slots 1–2, send brown into the VIP slot, then clear green and cyan into plus slots 1–2 before purple takes regular slot 3. Keep cyan in place between green and purple.

The Cyan Split in Level 53

A six-bus level that mixes open, VIP, and plus slots. The cyan in the middle of the plus-slot run is the color to keep straight.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Yellow and Red

  1. Read the front color — it is yellow, so the level opens on yellow.
  2. Send the yellow bus to regular slot 1 to collect the yellow group.
  3. Send the red bus to regular slot 2 to collect the red group.

Midgame: Brown Into the VIP Slot

  1. Confirm brown is next in the queue.
  2. Send the brown bus into the VIP slot. VIP collects the brown group and opens the plus-slot run.
  3. Line up green and cyan for the plus slots.

Finishing: Green, Cyan, Purple

  1. Send the green bus to plus slot 1 to collect the green group.
  2. Send the cyan bus to plus slot 2 — do not drop cyan between green and purple.
  3. Send the purple bus to regular slot 3 to collect the closing purple group and clear the level.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 53 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 53?+

Clear yellow and red into regular slots 1–2, brown into the VIP slot, green and cyan into plus slots 1–2, then purple into regular slot 3.

Which bus uses the VIP slot in Bus Traffic Fever Level 53?+

Brown. It comes third, after yellow and red clear regular slots 1–2.

Where do green and cyan go in Bus Traffic Fever Level 53?+

Into plus slots 1–2 — green first, then cyan — before purple takes regular slot 3.

Why is cyan important in Bus Traffic Fever Level 53?+

Because it sits between green and purple, and skipping it strands the cyan passengers and jams the level.

What trips players up in Bus Traffic Fever Level 53?+

Dropping cyan from the plus-slot run and firing purple before the cyan passengers board.

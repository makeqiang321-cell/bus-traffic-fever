Bus Traffic Fever Level 6 Guide: The Hidden Bus

Level 6 hides the matching bus behind a different color. Scan the exposed buses, clear the one blocking the color the queue wants, and the hidden bus opens up.

The Hidden Bus in Level 6

A board where the bus you need is parked behind a vehicle of another color. The level tests whether you scan for the matching bus and clear the path to it, not just the first thing you see.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Scan for the Match

  1. Read the front color, then scan the whole board for it.
  2. Note whether that bus is exposed or blocked.
  3. If blocked, identify the bus in front of it.

Midgame: Clear the Block

  1. Dispatch the blocking bus only if its color is also due.
  2. Free the slot the matching bus needs.
  3. Now send the matching bus to the open slot.

Finishing: Reach the Hidden Bus

  1. Once the path is clear, dispatch the matching bus.
  2. Board the group and free the space.
  3. Confirm no hidden bus remains before you finish.

Quick hint:

Scan the whole board for the color the queue wants, not just the nearest bus.

Common mistake:

Sending the nearest exposed bus because it is convenient, then finding the matching bus is still buried.

Pros and Cons

Pros. Builds the scan-before-dispatch habit that later levels depend on.

Cons. The hidden bus is easy to miss when the exposed one looks ready.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 6 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 6?+

Scan for the bus hidden behind others and match its color to the front group. Clearing the bus in front reveals it.

How long should Bus Traffic Fever Level 6 take?+

Under a minute once you spot the hidden bus. It is a scanning level, not a speed level.

Is the hidden bus in Bus Traffic Fever Level 6 always in the same spot?+

Yes, the layout is fixed, so the covered bus sits in the same position each run.

Why does my Bus Traffic Fever Level 6 board look busier than the video?+

A wrong dispatch leaves the front bus in place, so the hidden bus never surfaces. Match the front group and it appears.

Is there a trick to spotting the hidden bus in Bus Traffic Fever Level 6?+

Trace from the front group's color. Whichever bus of that color is parked out of reach is the one hiding behind the front bus.

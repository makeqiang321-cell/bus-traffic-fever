Bus Traffic Fever Level 6 Guide: The Hidden Freebie

Level 6 rewards a quick scan over a busy-looking board. Find the bus that can already move, pull it first, and re-scan after every move instead of tapping in order.

The Hidden Freebie in Level 6

A board that looks packed but is actually thin. Several buses sit in the way, but one exposed vehicle is already pointed at an open exit — everything else just follows it.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Find the already-free bus first. The board looks busier than it is, and the moment you pull that one exposed vehicle, the rest collapses behind it.

First Moves: Scanning Before Tapping

  1. Sweep your eyes over the lot once before you tap anything.
  2. Trace each exposed bus to the exit to find the free one.
  3. Pull the bus with the clear, unblocked path.

Midgame: Finding the Next Free Bus

  1. After the freebie leaves, re-scan the whole lot again.
  2. Look for the new bus that now has an unblocked path.
  3. Move that bus instead of grabbing whatever is closest.

Finishing: Re-Scanning After Every Pull

  1. After the first pull, look for the next already-free bus.
  2. Move it instead of tapping the closest vehicle.
  3. Repeat the scan until the board is clear.

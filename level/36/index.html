Bus Traffic Fever Level 36 Guide: The Final Split

Level 36 opens with green, then runs blue and red twice each with yellow between them. The sequence is green, blue, red, yellow, blue, and red to finish.

The Final Split in Level 36

A board where blue and red each come back for a second group. The queue runs green, blue, red, yellow, blue, red, and the two returns are the finisher.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Follow the order — green, blue, red, yellow, blue, red. Clear each group when it reaches the front, including both returns.

First Moves: Open With Green

  1. Green opens the run — clear it first.
  2. Blue is next, so take the first blue group.
  3. Red follows; board the red group.

Midgame: Clear Yellow, Then the Returns

  1. Yellow is queued — clear it.
  2. Blue returns, so take the second blue group.
  3. Re-read the front for the closing red.

Finishing: Close With Red

  1. Red closes the board.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Quick hint:

Clear in order — green, blue, red, yellow, blue, red — and catch both returns.

Common mistake:

Batching the two blues or two reds together, which skips yellow and jams the middle.

Pros and Cons

Pros. Two returning colors give the run a clear split structure.

Cons. The two returns make the middle easy to miscount.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 36 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 36?+

Clear the final split in order: green, blue, red, yellow, blue, red.

What is the split in Bus Traffic Fever Level 36?+

Blue and red each come back, so the back half splits your attention between two repeated colors.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 36 not finish?+

Because the second blue or red is grabbed early. Hold green, blue, red, yellow, blue, red.

Which bus do I serve first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 36?+

Green, then blue, red, yellow, blue, red.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 36 different from the other placement levels?+

Yes. The split of repeated blue and red at the end is its signature.

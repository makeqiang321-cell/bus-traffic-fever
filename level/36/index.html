Bus Traffic Fever Level 36 Guide: The Final Placement Split

Level 36 gives the final bus two possible exits. Choose the exit that lines up square, then place the bus into it cleanly so the board registers the finish.

The Final Placement Split in Level 36

A board where the final bus faces two exits. One lines up square and the other is half a lane off; the solution is choosing the right exit and placing the bus precisely.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Pick the square exit and place the last bus cleanly. The split is a choice, and only the lined-up exit locks the level.

First Moves: Clearing Both Exit Options

  1. Clear the buses in front of both exits.
  2. Leave the final bus free to choose.
  3. Do not commit until both options are open.

Midgame: Choosing the Square Exit

  1. Compare the two exits side by side.
  2. Pick the one that lines the bus up square.
  3. Align the final bus to that exit.

Finishing: Placing It Square

  1. Slide the final bus into the square exit.
  2. Stop the moment it locks in place.
  3. Confirm the square exit locked and clear the level.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 36 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 36?+

Clear both exits, pick the one that lines the final bus up square, and slide it in cleanly. The off-center exit never locks the finish.

What is the split in Bus Traffic Fever Level 36?+

The final bus faces two exits, and only one lines it up square. Choosing the right exit and placing it cleanly is the whole solve.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 36 not finish?+

Because the final bus went into the off-center exit. Pick the square exit and slide the bus in precisely.

Which exit do I use in Bus Traffic Fever Level 36?+

The one that lines the last bus up square. Compare both, then place the bus into the exit that locks cleanly.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 36 different from the other placement levels?+

Yes — it adds a choice between two exits, so picking the square one matters before the placement itself.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 48 Guide: The Triple Green

Level 48 hides one trap — three green buses that must clear back to back to back. Run orange into the VIP slot first, then all three greens through the regular slots, then red, teal, brown, and yellow into the plus slots.

The Triple Green in Level 48

An eight-vehicle level with a triple green run in the middle. Three green buses look identical, but each has one correct slot and they clear in a fixed order.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Orange Into the VIP Slot

  1. Read the front color — it is orange, so the level opens on orange.
  2. Send the orange bus to the VIP slot. VIP collects the first group and opens the regular slots.
  3. Confirm the next three groups are all green — this is the trap to count carefully.

Midgame: The Three Greens

  1. Send the first green bus to regular slot 1. Each green is its own passenger group.
  2. Send the second green bus to regular slot 2, then the third to regular slot 3.
  3. Do not treat the three greens as one — losing count strands the third green.

Finishing: Red and the Plus Slots

  1. Send the red bus to regular slot 4 to collect the red group.
  2. Send teal, brown, and yellow into plus slots 1–3 in order.
  3. Confirm all eight groups have boarded and the level is clear.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 48 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 48?+

Send orange into the VIP slot, then clear all three green buses in order, then red, teal, brown, and yellow into the plus slots.

Why are there three green buses in Bus Traffic Fever Level 48?+

Three green passenger groups are waiting, so three green buses have to clear back to back. Losing count strands the third one.

Where does orange go in Bus Traffic Fever Level 48?+

Into the VIP slot first. The three greens follow through the regular slots.

How many vehicles are in Bus Traffic Fever Level 48?+

Eight — orange, three greens, red, teal, brown, and yellow. The triple green is the key detail.

What trips players up in Bus Traffic Fever Level 48?+

Clearing only two of the three greens and moving red early, which jams the level.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 18 Guide: The Key Blocker

Level 18 places a key green bus at the heart of the board that cannot clear until the buses around it are gone. Work through yellow, red, green, blue, and pink, then the final green opens the exit.

The Key Blocker in Level 18

A board where a central bus blocks the exit and stays locked until its neighbors clear. The queue runs yellow, red, green, blue, pink, and green, and the last green is the key.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Clear the blockers in order — yellow, red, green, blue, pink — and the key green bus clears last. Do not try to force the key bus out before its path is open.

First Moves: Clear the Outer Blockers

  1. Yellow opens the run — clear it first.
  2. Red is next, so take the red group.
  3. Green comes up; board the green group.

Midgame: Free the Path

  1. Blue is queued next — clear it.
  2. Pink follows, so take the pink group.
  3. Check that the key bus now has a clear path.

Finishing: Release the Key

  1. Green returns last to open the way out.
  2. Board that last green group.
  3. Confirm the board is empty to finish.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 18 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

Remove the key bus by clearing in order: yellow, red, green, blue, pink, then green.

What if I can't find the blocker in Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

It is the bus parked in front of the others. Serve the queue and it is the first to go.

Can the blocker be in the back in Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

Sometimes, but the queue order still reaches it. Yellow opens, and the rest follows until it is free.

How do I find the blocker quickly in Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

Just follow yellow, red, green, blue, pink, green. The blocker clears as part of that order.

Why does removing the key bus matter so much in Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

Because it is parked in front of the group, so nothing else moves until the matching color clears it.

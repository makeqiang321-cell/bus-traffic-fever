Bus Traffic Fever Level 18 Guide: The Key Blocker

Level 18 is solved by finding the blocker. Spot the single bus everything else is stuck behind, free it only when a lane is ready, and the rest of the board opens at once.

The Key Blocker in Level 18

A mid board with one dominant blocker. Identify it and time its move correctly, and the whole board unlocks at once — the difficulty is spotting it and not moving it too early.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Find the bus everyone is stuck behind, then time its move. The blocker is not the bus that looks important — it is the one with the longest queue behind it.

First Moves: Finding the Blocker

  1. Count how many buses sit behind each vehicle.
  2. The one with the longest queue behind it is the blocker.
  3. Study the board until you are sure before moving.

Midgame: Preparing the Blocker's Path

  1. Once you have spotted the blocker, stop clearing random buses.
  2. Focus only on the vehicles that sit directly in the blocker's way.
  3. Do not touch the blocker itself until its whole path is clear.

Finishing: Timing the Blocker's Move

  1. Wait until a lane is open and the blocker's exit is clear.
  2. Free the blocker and let the whole board flow.
  3. Confirm the move timing matches the video.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 18 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

Identify the single bus that blocks the most vehicles, wait until a lane is ready, then free it to unlock the rest of the board.

What if I can't find the blocker in Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

Count the queue behind each bus. The one with the longest line of buses waiting behind it is the blocker, even if it looks unimportant.

Can the blocker be in the back row in Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

Yes — a blocker can sit anywhere. Look for the bus with the most vehicles behind it, not the one that looks prominent.

How do I find the blocker quickly in Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

Count how many buses sit behind each vehicle. The one with the longest queue behind it is the blocker, and the video's first few moves confirm it.

Why is timing the blocker's move so important in Bus Traffic Fever Level 18?+

Move it too early and you fill the one lane it needed. Wait until a lane is open and its exit is clear, then free it to unlock everything at once.

A full board with two returns — yellow, light blue, red, light blue, yellow, pink. Clear the returns in order and the board drains.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 28 Guide: The Full Board

Level 28 fills the board with six groups where yellow and light blue each return. The sequence is yellow, light blue, red, light blue, yellow, and pink to finish.

The Full Board in Level 28

A crowded board where two colors come back for a second group. The queue runs yellow, light blue, red, light blue, yellow, and pink, and the repeats are what make it busy.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Clear Yellow and Light Blue

  1. Yellow is up first — clear it.
  2. Light blue follows, so take that group.
  3. Red comes next; board the red group.

Midgame: Return to Light Blue and Yellow

  1. Light blue returns — clear the second light blue group.
  2. Yellow comes back around, so take the second yellow group.
  3. Re-read the front for the closing pink.

Finishing: Close With Pink

  1. Pink closes the board.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Quick hint:

Clear in order — yellow, light blue, red, light blue, yellow — then pink.

Common mistake:

Clearing both yellows or both light blues at once, which skips the queue and jams a slot.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The crowded board gives a real sense of draining once the returns are caught.

Cons. Two returning colors make the middle easy to misread.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 28 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 28?+

Order the full board: yellow, light blue, red, light blue, yellow, pink.

Why is Bus Traffic Fever Level 28 so hard?+

The full board with two repeated colors, yellow and light blue both come back, so tracking the queue is the challenge.

What order do I clear in Bus Traffic Fever Level 28?+

Yellow, light blue, red, light blue, yellow, pink, exactly, front to back.

How do I stop Bus Traffic Fever Level 28 from jamming?+

Serve the queue in order. Every wrong color leaves a bus parked where it blocks the rest.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 28 a known wall?+

It trips people up on the repeated light blue and yellow, but the sequence is fixed and solvable.

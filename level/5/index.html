Bus Traffic Fever Level 5 Guide: The Color Key

Level 5 closes the tutorial with the color mechanic. Read the groups before you move, route the bus that matches the most blocking group first, and let the collection chain do the rest.

The Color Key in Level 5

A board where passenger groups of different colors sit at different exits. The puzzle is no longer just bus positions — the order you collect the riders in is the thing that opens the lanes.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Clear the color group that blocks the most lanes first. Matching each bus to its group and collecting in the right order is what turns a stalled board into a chain reaction.

First Moves: Matching Bus to Group

  1. Read every color group before you touch the board.
  2. Identify the group that blocks the most lanes.
  3. Route the bus that matches that group first.

Midgame: Switching to the Next Color

  1. After the first group clears, find the next color in the chain.
  2. Route that group's bus before any off-sequence pickup.
  3. Resist collecting a closer group that is not next in line.

Finishing: Working Down the Chain

  1. Let the first group clear and the lane open.
  2. Move to the next most-blocking group in the chain.
  3. Confirm the collection order matches the video.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 5 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 5?+

Match each bus to its color group and clear the group that blocks the most lanes first. Watch the video for the exact color order.

What if I'm color-blind in Bus Traffic Fever Level 5?+

Match by position and the video's order rather than an exact shade. The group that blocks the most lanes is the first one to clear.

Does the color order change between attempts in Bus Traffic Fever Level 5?+

No, the board is fixed, so the correct collection order is the same every time. Replay the video if the groups look ambiguous.

Are the Bus Traffic Fever Level 5 colors the same in every game version?+

Usually, but some updates or device color settings can shift how they look. Match by position and the video's order rather than relying on an exact shade.

Why does my Bus Traffic Fever Level 5 board look busier than the video?+

Game versions can add or shuffle buses slightly. Focus on the color-group order in the video, since that rule stays the same even when the board differs.

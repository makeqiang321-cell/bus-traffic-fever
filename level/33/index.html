Bus Traffic Fever Level 33 Guide: The Inner Ring

Level 33 clears the outer buses to unlock the inner ring. The sequence is pink, light blue, green, blue, yellow, and purple, and each outer clear exposes more of the center.

The Inner Ring in Level 33

A board where the inner buses stay locked until the outer ring clears. The queue runs pink, light blue, green, blue, yellow, purple, and the inner ring opens as you go.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Clear Pink and Light Blue

  1. Pink is up first — clear it.
  2. Light blue follows, so take that group.
  3. Green comes next; board the green group.

Midgame: Unlock the Inner Ring

  1. Blue is queued — clear it.
  2. Yellow follows, so take the yellow group.
  3. Watch which inner bus the last clear exposes.

Finishing: Close With Purple

  1. Purple closes the board.
  2. Confirm no inner bus remains locked.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Quick hint:

Clear the outer ring first — pink, light blue, green, blue, yellow — then purple unlocks the center.

Common mistake:

Forcing an inner bus before its outer blocker has cleared, which has no path to a slot.

Pros and Cons

Bus Traffic Fever Level 33 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 33?+

Clear the inner ring in order: pink, light blue, green, blue, yellow, purple.

What is the inner-ring order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 33?+

Pink, light blue, green, blue, yellow, purple. The ring clears one slot at a time.

Why do buses stack in Bus Traffic Fever Level 33?+

Because a ring slot gets skipped. Serve the queue and each bus clears to its own slot.

Do I change pace in Bus Traffic Fever Level 33?+

No. Hold one steady read of pink, light blue, green, blue, yellow, purple and go down the list.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 33 harder than Level 27?+

Comparable. Light blue and blue sit close in the queue, so tell the two blues apart carefully.

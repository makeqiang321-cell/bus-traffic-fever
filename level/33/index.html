Bus Traffic Fever Level 33 Guide: The Conveyor Corridor

Level 33 runs the conveyor down a long corridor. Lock the conveyor's rhythm at the first bus and hold it the entire length, releasing each bus on the same rhythm until the corridor empties.

The Conveyor Corridor in Level 33

A conveyor that feeds a long corridor of buses in order. The rhythm is steady, but the challenge is maintaining the conveyor's rhythm across the full length without drifting.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Hold the conveyor's rhythm for the whole corridor. The level is an endurance test of the conveyor rhythm, one bus at a time.

First Moves: Locking the Corridor Rhythm

  1. Lock the conveyor's rhythm at the corridor's first bus.
  2. Release the corridor's opening buses on that rhythm.
  3. Commit the rhythm to memory before the corridor lengthens.

Midgame: Holding the Rhythm the Whole Length

  1. Hold the same rhythm down the long corridor.
  2. Do not drift faster or slower mid-length.
  3. Release each bus exactly on the conveyor's rhythm.

Finishing: Emptying the Corridor

  1. Keep the rhythm to the last few buses.
  2. Release the corridor's last bus on the same rhythm.
  3. Confirm the corridor is empty and clear.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 33 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 33?+

Lock the conveyor's rhythm at the first bus and hold it the whole corridor. Release each bus on the same rhythm until the line empties.

What is the conveyor corridor in Bus Traffic Fever Level 33?+

A long conveyor that feeds buses in order. The challenge is holding the conveyor's rhythm across the full length without drifting.

Why do buses stack in Bus Traffic Fever Level 33?+

Because the tap drifted off the conveyor's rhythm partway down the corridor. Lock back onto the rhythm and hold it steady.

Do I change pace in Bus Traffic Fever Level 33?+

No — keep one locked rhythm from the first bus to the last. The corridor rewards consistency, not speed.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 33 harder than Bus Traffic Fever Level 27?+

Different pressure — Level 27 speeds up mid-run, while Level 33 asks you to hold one rhythm over a longer stretch.

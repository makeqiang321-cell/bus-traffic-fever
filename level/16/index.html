Bus Traffic Fever Level 16 Guide: The Squeeze

Level 16 packs six buses into a tight grid. Dispatch blue first, then orange and red, and the two greens and final blue clear the remaining squeeze.

The Squeeze in Level 16

A board where the parking area is so tight that one wrong dispatch blocks the rest. The queue runs blue, orange, red, green, green, blue, and each clear frees a little more room.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Clear in queue order — blue, orange, red, green, green, blue. The tight grid punishes a wrong park, so keep each slot matched to the front color.

First Moves: Break the Squeeze

  1. Blue is first — clear the blue group.
  2. Orange follows, so take the orange group.
  3. Red comes next; board the red group.

Midgame: Work the Two Greens

  1. Green is up — clear the first green group.
  2. Green repeats, so take the second green group.
  3. Watch which bus the last clear exposes.

Finishing: Close With Blue

  1. Blue returns at the end to close the grid.
  2. Confirm nothing is still blocked.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 16 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 16?+

Clear the tight grid in order: blue, orange, red, green, green, blue.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 16 really easy, or is that a trick?+

The grid is tight but the sequence is short, blue, orange, red, green, green, blue, so it is quick once you read it.

How do I stop the board from tightening in Bus Traffic Fever Level 16?+

Dispatch in queue order. Every correct color frees a slot and stops the grid from closing in.

Why does my Bus Traffic Fever Level 16 board tighten even when I go fast?+

Speed does not help. A wrong color tightens it; only matching blue, orange, red, green, green, blue keeps it open.

How do I reopen a slot that just closed in Bus Traffic Fever Level 16?+

Serve the next color in the queue. Clearing the right bus opens the slot behind it again.

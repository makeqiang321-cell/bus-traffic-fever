Bus Traffic Fever Level 16 Guide: The Squeeze

Level 16 is forgiving until you let it tighten. Take the easy clears when they are there, and the moment a lane starts to close, reopen it before you push another bus through.

The Squeeze in Level 16

A quick board with a couple of spots that tighten under pressure. It is not about speed — recovery is the real skill, and rushing is exactly what packs the buses into the pinch.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Recover instead of forcing. The level clears fast unless you let it squeeze shut, so reopening a lane the moment it tightens is the whole skill.

First Moves: Taking the Easy Clears

  1. Take the easy clears while they are available.
  2. Watch for the moment a lane starts to tighten.
  3. Pause the second the board feels squeezed.

Midgame: Not Making the Squeeze Worse

  1. Once the easy clears are gone, stop hunting for a fast move.
  2. Choose the pull that keeps the most empty space on the board.
  3. Skip any move that pushes a bus into the tight spot.

Finishing: Reopening Before You Push

  1. Reopen a lane before you force another bus through.
  2. Back out the last blocking bus if you can.
  3. Resume only after you have space again.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 16 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 16?+

Take the easy clears, and the moment a lane starts to tighten, reopen it before forcing another move.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 16 really easy, or is that a trick?+

It is easy on paper, but it tightens under pressure — so it is a test of recovery, not a hard sequence. Slow down and it clears quickly.

How do I stop the board from tightening in Bus Traffic Fever Level 16?+

Stop rushing. Take the easy clears first and reopen a lane the moment it starts to close, rather than pushing through.

Why does my Bus Traffic Fever Level 16 board tighten even when I go fast?+

Speed is exactly what tightens it. Rushing packs buses into the pinch point, so slow down and reopen a lane before pushing through.

How do I reopen a lane that just closed in Bus Traffic Fever Level 16?+

Back out the last bus that blocked it if you can, or restart and leave that lane clear the next run. The board is short, so a restart is cheap.

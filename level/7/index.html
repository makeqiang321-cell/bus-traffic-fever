Bus Traffic Fever Level 7 Guide: The Chain Clear

Level 7 is a chain: clearing one bus exposes the next, and the queue tells you the color each step. Keep matching front to front and the board unravels.

The Chain Clear in Level 7

A board where buses are nested so that removing one reveals the next. The puzzle is reading the queue after every clear, since the available bus changes each time.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Start the Chain

  1. Read the front color and find its exposed bus.
  2. Dispatch it and watch which bus slides into the gap.
  3. Re-read the queue before the next move.

Midgame: Ride the Chain

  1. Each clear exposes a new bus; check its color against the queue.
  2. Dispatch the exposed bus that matches the front group.
  3. If two are exposed, pick the one the queue is calling for.

Finishing: Break the Chain Clean

  1. Keep re-reading after every dispatch.
  2. Clear the final exposed buses in queue order.
  3. Confirm the board is empty before you call it done.

Quick hint:

Re-read the queue after every clear — the exposed bus changes each time.

Common mistake:

Clearing from memory of the old board instead of re-reading, then dispatching a color that is no longer up.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The chain clears fast once you trust the queue over your memory.

Cons. A missed re-read sends the wrong bus and jams the chain.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 7 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 7?+

Dispatch in queue order and each clear reveals the next bus, forming a chain. Follow the front group and the chain runs end to end.

What if I can't see two moves ahead in Bus Traffic Fever Level 7?+

You don't have to. The queue spells out every move for you, one color at a time.

Does the chain order matter on Bus Traffic Fever Level 7's small board?+

Yes, the chain only runs if you match the front group each time. One wrong color snaps it.

Do I have to plan two moves ahead after Bus Traffic Fever Level 7 too?+

Not really. Every level after this one still just wants you to read the front group and dispatch its match.

What counts as a good first move in Bus Traffic Fever Level 7?+

The bus that matches the front group's color. There is only ever one correct first move.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 14 Guide: The Rear Guard

Level 14 hides its answer in the back row. Work the front just enough to expose the rear buses, clear them in the video's order, then return to the front.

The Rear Guard in Level 14

A board where the back row blocks everything but is hard to read until the front clears. The order you reveal the rear buses is the whole puzzle, not the front-row order.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Reveal the back row early and in order. The rear buses are the ones you actually need, and the level falls apart only when you expose them in the wrong sequence.

First Moves: Exposing the Back Row

  1. Clear only the front buses that block your view of the rear.
  2. Stop the moment the back row becomes visible.
  3. Switch your focus to the rear buses.

Midgame: Reading the Revealed Rear

  1. Once the back row is visible, stop and read it fully.
  2. Note which rear bus has a clear path and which is still blocked.
  3. Move the rear buses in the order the reveal suggests.

Finishing: Revealing the Rear in Order

  1. Reveal the rear buses in the video's exact order.
  2. Let the front row fall away after the back is open.
  3. Return to the front only once the rear is fully clear.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 14 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 14?+

Expose the back row early and reveal the rear buses in the video's order before returning to the front.

How do I expose the back row in Bus Traffic Fever Level 14 without over-clearing the front?+

Clear only the front buses that block your view of the rear, then stop. The moment you can see the back row, switch your focus there.

What if the back row is already visible in Bus Traffic Fever Level 14?+

Then go straight to revealing its buses in the video's order. The front row only matters as far as it blocks the rear.

How do I see the back row earlier in Bus Traffic Fever Level 14?+

Uncover it in small steps: move one front bus, glance at the rear, and stop the moment it comes into view. The earlier you can read the rear order, the safer the run.

Why does clearing the front row first fail in Bus Traffic Fever Level 14?+

It exposes the back row but leaves it in the wrong order, so the rear buses are stuck behind each other with no way to correct it.

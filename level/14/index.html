Bus Traffic Fever Level 14 Guide: The Rear Reveal

Level 14 runs six colors with green bookending the sequence. Start with the green bus, work through blue, purple, brown, and orange, and the final green clears the level.

The Rear Reveal in Level 14

A level where the rear buses stay hidden until the front ones clear. The queue runs green, blue, purple, brown, orange, and green, and each clear reveals the next vehicle.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Open With Green

  1. Green opens the run — clear it first.
  2. Blue is next, so take the blue group.
  3. Purple follows; board the purple group.

Midgame: Clear Brown and Orange

  1. Brown comes up next — clear it.
  2. Orange is queued, so take the orange group.
  3. Watch which rear bus the orange clear reveals.

Finishing: Close With Green

  1. Green returns at the end — board the last green group.
  2. Confirm no rear bus remains hidden.
  3. Finish once the level is empty.

Quick hint:

Clear front to back — green, blue, purple, brown, orange, then green — and let each clear reveal the next.

Common mistake:

Digging for a rear bus before the bus in front of it has cleared, which has no path to a slot.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The reveal mechanic makes each clear feel like progress.

Cons. Six colors means a lot to hold in mind, and the hidden rear is easy to misjudge.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 14 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 14?+

Dispatch in order to reveal the rear: green, blue, purple, brown, orange, then green again.

How do I expose the back buses in Bus Traffic Fever Level 14 without over-clearing the front?+

Just follow the queue, green, blue, purple, brown, orange, green, and the rear reveals itself as the front clears.

What if the back bus is already visible in Bus Traffic Fever Level 14?+

Still serve it only when its color reaches the front of the queue, never early.

How do I see the rear buses earlier in Bus Traffic Fever Level 14?+

You don't need to. The queue tells you the whole order up front, so the rear is no surprise.

Why does clearing the front first fail in Bus Traffic Fever Level 14?+

Because the front and rear alternate in the queue. Green opens, but blue and purple come next before you touch the other green.

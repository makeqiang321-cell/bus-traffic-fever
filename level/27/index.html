Bus Traffic Fever Level 27 Guide: The Conveyor Catch

Level 27 throws a faster conveyor at you. Lock the opening rhythm, then catch the speed-up the moment the conveyor changes, and release each bus on the new rhythm before it slips past.

The Conveyor Catch in Level 27

A conveyor board that changes pace partway through. The conveyor's rhythm accelerates, and the puzzle is catching the new tempo before a bus slips past the pickup point.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Catch the conveyor's speed-up and lock back onto the rhythm. The level is won by adjusting mid-run instead of holding one stale rhythm.

First Moves: Locking the Opening Rhythm

  1. Lock the conveyor's opening rhythm.
  2. Release the first buses on that steady rhythm.
  3. Watch for the moment the conveyor changes.

Midgame: Catching the Speed-Up

  1. Spot the exact point where the conveyor accelerates.
  2. Shift your tap to the faster rhythm immediately.
  3. Do not keep the old rhythm as buses slip past.

Finishing: Locking Back onto the Faster Rhythm

  1. Hold the faster rhythm through the final buses.
  2. Release the last bus on the new tempo.
  3. Confirm the faster conveyor is empty at the finish.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 27 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 27?+

Lock the opening rhythm, then catch the speed-up the moment the conveyor accelerates and lock back onto the faster tempo. Adjust mid-run instead of holding one stale rhythm.

What makes Bus Traffic Fever Level 27's conveyor different?+

It changes pace partway through. The conveyor accelerates in the middle, so you have to catch the faster rhythm or buses slip past.

Why do buses slip past in Bus Traffic Fever Level 27?+

Because you kept the opening rhythm after the conveyor sped up. Shift to the faster tempo the instant the conveyor changes.

Do I need to watch the conveyor the whole time in Bus Traffic Fever Level 27?+

Yes — the speed-up comes without warning. Watch for the change and match the new rhythm before any bus slips past.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 27 harder than Bus Traffic Fever Level 21's conveyor?+

Bus Traffic Fever Level 19 Guide: The Two-Lane Waltz

Level 19 makes you juggle two lanes at once. Treat them as one puzzle, alternate moves between them, and never finish one side while the other is left blocked behind you.

The Two-Lane Waltz in Level 19

A board where two parallel lanes share the exits. Keeping both alive at once is the puzzle, so committing to one lane and letting the other seal shut is the failure state.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Balance the two lanes — never finish one while the other is blocked. Alternating moves the way the video does is what keeps both routes alive at once.

First Moves: Tracking Both Lanes

  1. Track both lanes at once from the first move.
  2. Move one bus per lane, then switch.
  3. Mirror the video's balancing rhythm.

Midgame: Correcting the Balance

  1. Watch for one lane pulling ahead of the other.
  2. If a lane starts to clear too fast, hold it back a move.
  3. Give the slower lane the next bus to keep the two even.

Finishing: Finishing Together

  1. Keep alternating until both lanes are nearly clear.
  2. Finish the last buses without leaving one side blocked.
  3. Confirm the balancing order matches the video.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 19 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 19?+

Alternate moves between the two lanes so neither closes while you work the other, matching the video's balancing rhythm.

How do I split my attention between two lanes in Bus Traffic Fever Level 19?+

Move one bus per lane, then switch. Use the video's rhythm as a metronome rather than trying to solve one side to completion.

What happens if one lane finishes first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 19?+

If the other lane is left blocked, you are stuck — so finish them together. Never leave one side sealed while you complete the other.

Can I clear one lane completely first in Bus Traffic Fever Level 19?+

No — that is the trap. Finishing one side leaves the other blocked, so you must alternate moves and keep both lanes alive at once.

How do I keep track of both lanes in Bus Traffic Fever Level 19?+

Move one bus per lane, then switch. Watch the video's rhythm and mirror it rather than trying to solve one side to completion.

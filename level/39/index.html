Bus Traffic Fever Level 39 Guide: The Final Ring

Level 39 clears the final ring in order: purple, blue, green, orange, yellow, and orange to finish. The first orange clears the middle and the second orange ends the run.

The Final Ring in Level 39

A board where the queue circles the board and orange appears twice. The sequence is purple, blue, green, orange, yellow, orange, and the second orange is the finisher.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Follow the order — purple, blue, green, orange, yellow, orange. Clear the ring front to back, including the second orange at the end.

First Moves: Clear Purple and Blue

  1. Dispatch the purple bus to the first slot to board the purple group.
  2. Dispatch the blue bus to the first slot to clear the blue group.
  3. Dispatch the green bus to the first slot as green reaches the front.

Midgame: Work the Middle Orange

  1. Dispatch the orange bus to the third slot to clear the orange group.
  2. Dispatch the yellow bus to the second slot to board the yellow group.
  3. Re-read the queue for the closing orange.

Finishing: Close With Orange

  1. Dispatch the final orange bus to the third slot to board the orange group.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Quick hint:

Clear the ring in order — purple, blue, green, orange, yellow — then the second orange.

Common mistake:

Clearing both oranges together, which skips the yellow group in the middle.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The circling queue gives the final ring a clean, predictable flow.

Cons. The second orange is easy to miss after the busy middle.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 39 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 39?+

Clear the final ring: purple, blue, green, orange, yellow, orange.

What is the ring order in Bus Traffic Fever Level 39?+

Purple, blue, green, orange, yellow, orange. Orange comes back at the very end.

Why do buses stack in Bus Traffic Fever Level 39?+

Because the second orange is missed. Hold purple, blue, green, orange, yellow, orange to the finish.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 39 an endurance test?+

It is. A full six-deep ring with a repeated orange at the end asks for steady queue reading.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 39 the last ring level?+

It leads into the final board, so treat the repeated orange as the last real test of tracking.

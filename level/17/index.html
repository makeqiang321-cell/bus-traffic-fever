Bus Traffic Fever Level 17 Guide: The Wrong Order

Level 17 alternates blue and pink twice before finishing on brown and orange. There is a tempting shortcut that looks faster, but it only works if you skip the queue — which jams the board.

The Wrong Order in Level 17

A board where two colors trade places in the queue, so any attempt to batch-clear them breaks the order. The level is a test of discipline: clear exactly what the queue asks, no shortcuts.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Follow the alternating order — blue, pink, blue, pink, brown, orange. Do not batch the two blues or two pinks; clear each group when it reaches the front.

First Moves: Start the Alternation

  1. Dispatch the first blue bus to board the blue group.
  2. Dispatch the pink bus to clear the pink group.
  3. Dispatch the second blue bus as blue returns.

Midgame: Hold the Order

  1. Dispatch the second pink bus when pink comes back.
  2. Re-read the queue before the closing colors.
  3. Keep a slot open for brown.

Finishing: Brown, Then Orange

  1. Dispatch the brown bus to board the brown group.
  2. Dispatch the orange bus to clear the orange group.
  3. Confirm the board is empty to finish.

Quick hint:

Clear the alternation in order — blue, pink, blue, pink — then brown and orange, no batching.

Common mistake:

Batching both blues or both pinks to save a move, which skips the queue and jams a slot.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 17 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 17?+

Hold the order exactly: blue, pink, blue, pink, brown, orange. The level punishes any wrong-order dispatch.

How fast should I actually go in Bus Traffic Fever Level 17?+

Slow and steady. This level is about order, and speed just makes you grab the wrong color.

Does a timer matter in Bus Traffic Fever Level 17?+

There is no timer. The only thing that matters is the sequence blue, pink, blue, pink, brown, orange.

Is speed ever the right call in Bus Traffic Fever Level 17?+

No. The wrong-order level rewards patience, not speed.

Why does slowing down make Bus Traffic Fever Level 17 faster?+

Because a wrong dispatch forces a restart. Reading the order first finishes it in one clean run.

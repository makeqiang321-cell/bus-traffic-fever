Bus Traffic Fever Level 35 Guide: The Rush Fix

Level 35 bookends the queue with red: red, purple, yellow, green, light blue, and red to finish. The first red clears the bottleneck and the last red ends the run.

The Rush Fix in Level 35

A level where red appears at both ends of the queue. The sequence is red, purple, yellow, green, light blue, red, and the two reds are separate groups.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Clear the First Red

  1. Red is up first — clear the opening red group.
  2. Purple follows, so take the purple group.
  3. Yellow comes next; board the yellow group.

Midgame: Work Green and Light Blue

  1. Green is queued — clear it.
  2. Light blue follows, so take that group.
  3. Re-read the front for the closing red.

Finishing: Close With Red

  1. Red returns at the end to close.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the level is empty.

Quick hint:

Clear in order — red, purple, yellow, green, light blue — then the final red.

Common mistake:

Clearing both reds at the start, then finding the final group has no red bus left to board it.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The bookended red gives the run a clear start and finish.

Cons. Easy to over-clear red early and strand the closing group.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 35 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 35?+

Fix the rush in order: red, purple, yellow, green, light blue, red.

What is the rush correction in Bus Traffic Fever Level 35?+

The final red. The level rushes at the end, so hold red, purple, yellow, green, light blue, red to the finish.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 35 stall at the busiest moment?+

Because red comes back at the end and players grab the wrong red. Serve the queue straight through.

Do I reset Bus Traffic Fever Level 35 when it locks?+

If a wrong color went in, yes. Restart and hold the full order.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 35 the hardest correction level?+

It is up there. The repeated red at the end punishes losing track late in the run.

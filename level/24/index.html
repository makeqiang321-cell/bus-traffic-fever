Bus Traffic Fever Level 24 Guide: The Final-Spot Squeeze

Level 24 hangs on the last bus. Clear the path so the final bus has a clean lane, then place it square in its exit without over-sliding, and the board is done.

The Final-Spot Squeeze in Level 24

A board that feels easy right up until the last move. The final bus must settle exactly into its exit, and a mistake of half a lane at the end is the only way to fail.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Clear the path, then park the last bus perfectly. The level is decided entirely by that final placement.

First Moves: Clearing a Lane for the Last Bus

  1. Clear the buses in front of the final bus first.
  2. Leave the final bus's exit lane completely open.
  3. Do not commit the final bus until its lane is clean.

Midgame: Lining the Final Bus Up

  1. Square the final bus up with its exit lane.
  2. Adjust the angle before the last slide.
  3. Check the gap is exactly one lane wide.

Finishing: Placing It Without Over-Sliding

  1. Guide the last bus in with one smooth motion.
  2. Stop the instant it is square in the exit.
  3. Check the square parking and clear the level.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 24 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 24?+

Clear the final bus's lane first, then place it square in its exit. The last move is the whole solve, so line it up before you slide.

What is 'clean final placement' in Bus Traffic Fever Level 24?+

Parking the last bus exactly in its exit lane without over-sliding. The rest of the board is easy, but the final placement must be square.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 24 fail on the last bus?+

Because the final bus landed half a lane off its exit. Clear its lane fully, then slide it in one controlled motion.

Which part of Bus Traffic Fever Level 24 matters most?+

The final placement. Everything before it is just clearing a lane, so focus on parking the last bus square.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 24 the first final-placement level?+

Yes — it isolates the mechanic on an easy board so you learn to save your focus for the last bus.

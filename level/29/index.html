Bus Traffic Fever Level 29 Guide: The Mid Reset

Level 29 runs six distinct colors with no repeats: orange, yellow, purple, green, blue, and red. The level rewards re-reading the queue after every single clear.

The Mid Reset in Level 29

A board where all six colors appear exactly once, so there is no pattern to lean on. The queue runs orange, yellow, purple, green, blue, red, and each clear changes what is up next.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

First Moves: Open With Orange

  1. Orange is up first — clear it.
  2. Yellow follows, so take the yellow group.
  3. Purple comes next; board the purple group.

Midgame: Clear Green and Blue

  1. Green is queued — clear it.
  2. Blue follows, so take the blue group.
  3. Re-read the front for the closing red.

Finishing: Close With Red

  1. Red finishes the run.
  2. Confirm no group is left waiting.
  3. Finish once the board is empty.

Quick hint:

No repeats here — clear orange, yellow, purple, green, blue, red in order and re-read each time.

Common mistake:

Clearing from memory of the first read, since with no repeats the next color changes every dispatch.

Pros and Cons

Pros. The no-repeat board is a clean test of re-reading under pressure.

Cons. With nothing to anchor to, a skipped re-read sends the wrong bus.

Bus Traffic Fever Level 29 FAQ

How do I beat Bus Traffic Fever Level 29?+

Reset the middle of the board: orange, yellow, purple, green, blue, red.

What is the correction point in Bus Traffic Fever Level 29?+

The middle, purple, green, and blue, where most runs stall if a color goes in early.

Why does Bus Traffic Fever Level 29 collapse after a clean start?+

Orange and yellow open clean, but skipping purple for green or blue collapses the board.

Do I restart Bus Traffic Fever Level 29 when it collapses?+

Yes, there is no undo, so restart and hold orange, yellow, purple, green, blue, red.

Is Bus Traffic Fever Level 29 like Level 23?+

It shares the mid-correction idea, but the colors and order are different: orange, yellow, purple, green, blue, red.
